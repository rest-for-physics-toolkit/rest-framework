Detailed Description

Event container for Geant4 output with flat AOD views and track synchronization.

The event of restG4: the primaries, the energy deposited per volume (and per volume, particle and process) and, with storeTracks, the tracks and their hits (TRestGeant4Track is a light view of a track of the event). All the data is in fEventData (TRestGeant4EventData); the names of the particle, process and volume IDs are in the TRestGeant4Metadata of the run (GetGeant4Metadata). An event with SubID > 0 is a sub-event (e.g. a delayed radioactive decay, subEventTimeDelay).

The constructor from a G4Event and InsertTrack, UpdateTrack, InsertStep and UpdatePrimaryData are implemented in restG4 (they need Geant4).

Draws the event, one row of pads per option. Options separated by ':':

  • graph[process1,process2,...]: the hits (also those without energy, the trajectories) in the XZ, YZ and XY projections, coloured by particle (TRestGeant4Track::GetParticleColor), the marker size growing with the energy; the hits of the given Geant4 processes (e.g. phot, compt, eBrem) are marked with open black markers, with a legend of particles and processes;
  • hist(rootOption)[binSize]: histograms weighted by energy of the hits with energy (TRestHits::DrawHistograms): projections, 3D view and X, Y and Z distributions; binSize in mm, 3 by default (as legacy);
  • print: PrintEvent. Default: "graph:hist(Cont0,colz)", as legacy graphXZ:graphYZ:histXZ(Cont0,colz):histYZ(Cont0,colz).

void TRestGeant4Event::RebuildSecondaries ( )

Fills the secondaries of each track (trackSecondariesIDs, Index and Offsets) from the parent IDs: the IDs of the children of the track n, in storage order, are trackSecondariesIDs[i] for i in [trackSecondariesIndices[n], trackSecondariesIndices[n] + trackSecondariesOffsets[n]). The children of a track are not consecutive while simulating, restG4 calls it when the event is submitted.
