Detailed Description

Converts the energy deposits of a TRestDetectorHitsEvent into ionization electrons and drifts them to the boundary plane above the readout with a drift response computed by restGarfield in a field map (TRestDetectorDriftResponse): the drift in a non-uniform field, for special simulations, instead of TRestDetectorHitsDriftProcess (uniform field).

The parameters (YAML keys) and an example are described in the class documentation (TRestDetectorHitsDriftResponseProcess.cxx).

Author
JuanAn García juana.nosp@m.ngp@.nosp@m.uniza.nosp@m.r.es

Converts the energy of each hit into ionization electrons (energy / wValue of the medium, with the chosen fluctuations, as TRestDetectorHitsDriftProcess) and drifts each electron with a drift response in a non-uniform field (TRestDetectorDriftResponse, computed by restGarfield from a field map, responseFile) to the boundary plane above the readout, where the amplification starts: for special simulations with a field map, instead of TRestDetectorHitsDriftProcess (uniform field, the driftField of the medium).

Each electron survives with the fraction of the electrons that reach the boundary from the position of its hit (TRestDetectorDriftResponse::GetSurvival, interpolated between the nodes of the grid); a surviving electron becomes a hit on the readout (the hit projected on the readout plane, TRestDetectorReadout::ProjectToReadout, plus the displacement sampled from the response along the axes of the readout plane, TRestDetectorDriftResponse::PlaneAxes of the readout normal) at the time of the hit plus the drift time sampled from the response (SampleArrival), with the energy of its electrons as TRestDetectorHitsDriftProcess. Hits outside the grid of the response are removed, and a hit whose number of electrons fluctuates to 0 or less.

The arrival is on the readout plane at the time of the boundary plane: the drift from the boundary to the readout (through the mesh) belongs to the amplification.

Parameters: responseFile, readout (empty: the data readout of the TRestDetectorReadoutManager), medium (empty: the only one with charge), fluctuations ("fano", "poisson" or "none"), maxHits (1000, 0: no limit), seed (0: random seed; otherwise the generator is re-seeded at each event from the seed and the event and subevent IDs, TRestEvent::EventSeed, so the result does not depend on the number of threads).

Example:

driftResponse: class: "TRestDetectorHitsDriftResponseProcess" responseFile: "drift_response.root" maxHits: 1000
