Detailed Description

Detector readout implementation that imports geometry and channels from GDML naming rules.

The geometry is the GDML file of the simulation (e.g. the vetoes of restG4, read with TRestGDMLParser, so its entities and parameters are resolved as restG4 does). Each entry of readoutInfo selects the sensitive volumes whose geometry path matches the regular expression volume (one channel each, the first entry matching a path wins); their physical ID comes from the numbers captured by the expression:

  • two captured numbers n1, n2: offset + n1 * multiplier + n2 (multiplier 10 by default);
  • one captured number n: offset + n;
  • none: offset, incremented for each channel found (in the order of the geometry).

readoutVolume (optional) is the regular expression of the volume where the signal of each channel is collected (e.g. the light guide of a scintillator): exactly one in the same parent volume as the channel, otherwise BuildGeometry throws. It gives GetReadoutPosition and GetDistanceToReadout (used by the attenuation model of TRestDetectorLightCollectionProcess). The hits are not drifted: GetDistanceToReadout without channel is NaN.

Parameters: decodingFile and, in readoutParameters, gdmlFileName and readoutInfo (volume, readoutVolume, offset, multiplier). volumeToPhysicalIDMap and physicalIDToReadoutPath are written by BuildGeometry (not inputs).

readout: class: "TRestGDMLReadout" name: "Veto_Readout" decodingFile: "veto.dec" readoutParameters: gdmlFileName: "setup.gdml" readoutInfo:

  • volume: .*scintillatorVolume-* readoutVolume: scintillatorLightGuideVolume offset: 288
