Detailed Description

A virtual volume (box or sphere) of the volume biasing of restG4.

An entry of the biasingVolumes list of TRestGeant4Metadata. The volumes are not part of the Geant4 geometry: they are geometric tests (inside an axis-aligned box or a sphere) applied by restG4 to the steps of the particles. They must be nested and listed from the outermost to the innermost (each one strictly inside the previous one, without touching it, checked by TRestGeant4Metadata::CheckBiasingVolumes).

restG4 runs the simulation in stages (one per volume, plus a last one). In stage k a particle entering volume k is stopped and, if its kinetic energy is inside energyRange, its state at the crossing point is stored; stage k+1 simulates each stored particle biasingFactor times. Particles entering with an energy outside energyRange are stopped and not stored (their contribution is discarded). See TRestGeant4Metadata and the restG4 README for the stages and the weights. 

biasingVolumes:
  - type: "virtualSphere"     # virtualBox (default) or virtualSphere
    position: [0, 0, 0]       # center (mm)
    size: 50 cm               # box side or sphere radius (mm), > 0
    biasingFactor: 10         # integer >= 1: replicas of each particle stored
    energyRange: [0, 10 MeV]  # keV: particles stored (inclusive range)

Fraction t (0 to 1) of the segment from from to to (mm) where it enters the volume.

The segment is a straight line (the chord of a Geant4 step). Returns -1 if from is inside the volume, or if the segment does not enter it (also when it only touches its surface); otherwise the entry point is from + t (to - from), also when the segment goes through the volume and leaves it.
