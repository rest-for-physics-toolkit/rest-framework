Detailed Description

Adds simulated electronic noise to every signal of a TRestRawSignalEvent.

Each entry of noise (TRestNoiseParameters) is added in order to all the signals, the samples clamped to the short range:

  • white: gaussian noise of sigma amplitude, independent for each bin and signal.
  • sin (or sinusoidal): a sine of period period bins with the same random phase for all the signals of the event, and a slowly modulated random envelope of maximum amplitude.

seed: seed of the random generator (0, the default: a random seed). Otherwise the generator is re-seeded at each event from the seed and the event and subevent IDs (TRestEvent::EventSeed), so the noise does not depend on the number of threads.

No observables. Example:

addNoise:
class: "TRestRawSignalAddNoiseProcess"
noise:
- method: "white"
amplitude: 15
- method: "sin"
amplitude: 100
period: 55
seed: 1
