Detailed Description

Light response of a scintillating medium: the light produced by the energy deposits (used by TRestDetectorScintillationProcess) and its propagation to the photocathode in a transparent scintillator (attenuation model of TRestDetectorLightCollectionProcess). It is the light key of a TRestDetectorMedium (no class key). The quenching is done on the Geant4 deposits (TRestGeant4QuenchingProcess); the local capture of an opaque scintillator and the quantum efficiency are parameters of TRestDetectorLightCollectionProcess.

Parameters:

  • lightYield: scintillation photons produced per keV of (quenched) deposited energy.
  • attenuationLength: the deposited energy reaching the readout is E exp(-d / attenuationLength), with d the distance to the readout of the channel (TRestDetectorReadout::GetDistanceToReadout).
  • effectiveLightSpeed: the signal is delayed by d / effectiveLightSpeed (effective speed along the scintillator, with its reflections).

  • scintillation: time profiles of the emission of the photons (TRestDetectorScintillationProfile), per particle (Particle ID of the hits, TRestDetectorHitsEvent::GetParticleID), e.g. for pulse shape discrimination; at most one without particles (the default). A particle without profile (and without default) is emitted at once.

    light: lightYield: 10 attenuationLength: 3 m effectiveLightSpeed: 150 mm/ns scintillation:

    • components: # default
      • {fraction: 0.8, riseTime: 0.9 ns, decayTime: 2.1 ns}
      • {fraction: 0.2, decayTime: 20 ns}
    • particles: ["alpha", "proton", "ion"] components:
      • {fraction: 0.5, decayTime: 2.1 ns}
      • {fraction: 0.5, decayTime: 60 ns}
