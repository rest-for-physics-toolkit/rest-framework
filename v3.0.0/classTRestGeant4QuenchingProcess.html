Detailed Description

Quenching of the energy deposits of a TRestGeant4Event: the energy of the hits in the selected volumes is replaced by the visible one, and the deposited energies of the event (total, per volume, per volume, particle and process, per track and in the sensitive volumes) are recomputed from the hits.

Models, per volume:

  • lindhard: the hit energy is multiplied by the Lindhard factor of the hadronic target nucleus of the hit (A, Z), for the hits with a target (A > 0). It needs the simulation with storeHadronicTargetInfo: true; otherwise InitProcess throws (a configuration error).
  • birks: Birks law, factor 1 / (1 + kB dE/dx) with dE/dx the hit energy over the step length (the distance to the previous hit of the track in the same volume, birksFallbackStepLength for the first one). Not applied to gamma, opticalphoton, geantino, chargedgeantino and neutrinos.

Only the volumes given are quenched, each one with its model (required): those of volume and, with model, all the other volumes of the geometry; the rest keep their energy. 

quenching:
  class: "TRestGeant4QuenchingProcess"
  birksConstant: 0.000126            # mm/keV, default of the volumes
  birksFallbackStepLength: 0.5 mm    # default of the volumes
  applyToHitEnergies: true           # false: the hits keep their energy
  model: "lindhard"                  # optional: the other volumes of the geometry
  volume:
    - name: "gasVolume"
      model: "lindhard"
    - name: "^scintillatorVolume"
      model: "birks"
      birksConstant: 0.0001      # mm/keV
  observables: ["all"]

Observables: sensitiveVolumeEnergyBefore and sensitiveVolumeEnergyAfter (keV, energy in the sensitive volumes before and after the quenching) and sensitiveQuenched (1 if they differ by more than 0.01 keV, 0 otherwise). It throws at initialization if the run has no TRestGeant4Metadata or if a volume uses the lindhard model and the simulation has no storeHadronicTargetInfo; a volume entry without model is a configuration error; an expression without matching volume is reported as an error and skipped.
