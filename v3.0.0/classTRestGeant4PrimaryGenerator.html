Detailed Description

Samples the primaries of an event from a TRestGeant4PrimaryGeneratorInfo: one per thread, with its own random generator and copies of the spatial generator and the sources. The positions in GDML volumes need the Geant4 geometry, they are given by SampleGdmlPosition, which restG4 implements. Units: keV, mm. The generator options (YAML) and examples are documented in TRestGeant4PrimaryGenerator.cxx.

Primary generator of restG4: samples, for each event, the primary particles (name, excitation, charge, kinetic energy, direction and origin) described by the generator section of TRestGeant4Metadata. Units: keV, mm and rad; any value can be given with units (e.g. 5.3 MeV, 2 cm, 30 deg).

Structure

Each section has a type, which selects the class that reads it; each class reads only its own fields with UpdateParamsFromYAML, checks them (an error with a message if a needed one is missing or not valid) and writes them back with UpdateYAMLFromParams, so the stored metadata has the values used. 

generator:                      TRestGeant4PrimaryGeneratorInfo
  type: "..."                   TRestGeant4SpatialGenerator: point, volume, surface, cosmic or cry
  ...                           its fields
  spatialDensityFunction: "..." optional
  source:                       TRestGeant4ParticleSource, one or a list
    particle: "..."
    energy:                     TRestGeant4EnergyDistribution
      type: "..."               mono, flat, log, formula, TH1D (or formula2, TH2D, cosmic)
    angular:                    TRestGeant4AngularDistribution
      type: "..."               flux, isotropic, formula, TH1D (or formula2, TH2D, cosmic)

The types are case insensitive. All the sources of an event start at the same position (one primary vertex per particle).

Generator type (TRestGeant4SpatialGenerator)

  • point (TRestGeant4SpatialGeneratorPoint): position: [x, y, z].
  • volume (TRestGeant4SpatialGeneratorVolume): uniform inside
    • a GDML volume, from: "name" (a physical volume, or a logical one with a single physical volume), excluding its daughters; its placement in the world is taken from the geometry;
    • or a shape: box (size: [x, y, z]), sphere (size: [radius, 0, 0]) or cylinder (size: [radius, length, 0], axis Z).

  • surface (TRestGeant4SpatialGeneratorSurface): on the surface of

    • a GDML volume, from: "name" (G4VSolid::GetPointOnSurface, not uniform for every solid);
    • or a shape: sphere (size: [radius, 0, 0]), cylinder (size: [radius, length, 0], lateral surface, axis Z), circle (size: [radius, 0, 0], XY plane) or wall (size: [x, y, 0], XY plane). On a sphere, as legacy, the angular direction is replaced by the one to its center.

    The shapes are centered at position ([0, 0, 0] by default) and rotated by rotationValue (angle) around rotationAxis ([0, 0, 1] by default).

  • cosmic (TRestGeant4SpatialGeneratorCosmic): the direction is sampled first and the origin is a uniform point of the disk of radius radius through the world center normal to that direction, moved back to the sphere of that radius, so the flux through any surface inside the sphere is the one of the distributions. radius is, if not given, the one of the sphere circumscribing the world box (written to the stored metadata); the generation surface, to normalize to a flux, is pi radius^2 (TRestGeant4Metadata::GetCosmicGeneratorSurfaceCm2). It needs a single source, not a Decay0 one.
  • cry (TRestGeant4SpatialGeneratorCRY): the cosmic-ray showers of CRY, generated during the simulation (restG4 built with CRY), one per event, with all their particles (name, charge, energy, position, direction and time relative to the first one); it has no source. The options of CRY (subBoxLength, latitude, altitude, maxExposureTime...) are in their own section of the TRestGeant4Manager, of class TRestGeant4CRY; the generator only places the CRY square: its horizontal plane is centered at position ([0, 0, 0] by default) and the CRY +Z (up) is verticalAxis ([0, 1, 0] by default: the cosmics go down along -Y). The square must be inside the world, restG4 stops with an error otherwise. The seed of CRY is the metadata seed; the threads share the CRY generator. The simulation stops at nEvents or, with maxExposureTime, when CRY has simulated that time (with nEvents 0, only then). At the end, the generator section stores showersUsed and exposureTime, the time simulated by CRY (TRestGeant4Metadata::GetCRYExposureTime), to normalize to a rate.

spatialDensityFunction (volume and surface) is a TF3 formula of x, y, z (mm, world coordinates) with values in [0, 1]: each position is accepted with that probability (an error if it is > 1).

Source (TRestGeant4ParticleSource)

  • particle: Geant4 name (e.g. "alpha", "e-", "gamma", "neutron", "mu-") or ion name (e.g. "Pb210"), with excitedLevel (excitation energy, 0 by default) and charge (in units of e, 0 by default).
  • energy and angular, below.
  • Or only use: "file.dat": the particles (gamma, e-, e+ and alpha, with their energy and direction) of a random event of a Decay0 file.

Energy type (TRestGeant4EnergyDistribution)

  • mono: energy.
  • flat: uniform in range: [min, max].
  • log: uniform in log(E) in range (min > 0).
  • formula: the named formula (CosmicNeutrons, FissionNeutronsU238, EnvironmentalGammas or EnvironmentalNeutrons) in range (intersected with the formula one, an error if they do not overlap), sampled with formulaNPoints (5000 by default).
  • TH1D: the histogram nameInFile of filename, the bins with the center in range; the x axis is in MeV or GeV if its title says so, otherwise in keV; uniform inside the bin.

Angular type (TRestGeant4AngularDistribution)

All of them have direction ([0, -1, 0] by default): the fixed direction or the reference of the angle (the azimuth around it is uniform).

  • flux: the fixed direction.
  • isotropic: uniform in 4 pi or, with isotropicConeHalfAngle, in the solid angle of the cone around direction.
  • formula: the angle to direction follows the named formula (Cos2, Cos3 or Sin2theta, 0 above 90 deg; its density in the angle, not per solid angle) in range ([0, 180 deg] by default), sampled with formulaNPoints (500 by default).
  • TH1D: the angle to direction, in rad, from the histogram nameInFile of filename, the bins with the center in range.

Energy and angle together

When energy and angular have the same type formula2, TH2D or cosmic, the energy and the angle to the angular direction are sampled together (each section gives its range; formula, filename and nameInFile can be in either):

  • formula2: the named formula (CosmicMuons: energy in keV, zenith angle in rad), sampled with the energy and angular formulaNPoints.
  • TH2D: the histogram nameInFile of filename, energy (x) in MeV and angle (y) in deg.
  • cosmic: as TH2D, the histogram <nameInFile>_energy_zenith, nameInFile being the particle key (neutron, proton, gamma, electron_minus, electron_plus, muon_minus, muon_plus, neutron_below_1MeV, neutron_between_1MeV_and_10GeV, neutron_above_10GeV), which gives the particle if not given. Each bin is divided by cos(angle): the flux through a horizontal surface to the one of the cosmic generator.

Examples

Alphas from a thin source layer of the geometry: 

generator:
  type: "volume"
  from: "source"
  source:
    particle: "alpha"
    angular:
      type: "isotropic"
    energy:
      type: "mono"
      energy: 5.3 MeV

Pencil beam of electrons: 

generator:
  type: "point"
  position: [0.0, 0.0, -200.0 mm]
  source:
    particle: "e-"
    angular:
      type: "flux"
      direction: [0.0, 0.0, 1.0]
    energy:
      type: "mono"
      energy: 30.0 keV

Cosmic neutrons: 

generator:
  type: "cosmic"
  source:
    particle: "neutron"
    angular:
      type: "formula"
      formula: "Cos2"
      direction: [0, -1, 0]
      formulaNPoints: 1000
    energy:
      type: "formula"
      formula: "CosmicNeutrons"
      formulaNPoints: 10000
      range: [10 MeV, 10 GeV]

Cosmic muons with correlated energy and zenith angle: 

generator:
  type: "cosmic"
  radius: 2 m
  source:
    particle: "mu-"
    angular:
      type: "formula2"
      formula: "CosmicMuons"
      direction: [0, -1, 0]
      range: [0, 90 deg]
    energy:
      type: "formula2"

Cosmic flux from a file of energy-zenith histograms: 

generator:
  type: "cosmic"
  source:
    angular:
      type: "cosmic"
      direction: [0, -1, 0]
    energy:
      type: "cosmic"
      filename: "cosmics.root"
      nameInFile: "neutron"

Cosmic-ray showers of CRY (with a TRestGeant4CRY section in the TRestGeant4Manager), the CRY square 1 m above the world center: 

generator:
  type: "cry"
  position: [0, 1, 0 m]
  verticalAxis: [0, 1, 0]

Gammas from a rotated circle, in a cone around its normal, and a Decay0 event at the same point: 

generator:
  type: "surface"
  shape: "circle"
  size: [5 cm, 0, 0]
  position: [0, 0, 10 cm]
  rotationAxis: [1, 0, 0]
  rotationValue: 30 deg
  source:
    - particle: "gamma"
      angular:
        type: "isotropic"
        direction: [0, 0, -1]
        isotropicConeHalfAngle: 20 deg
      energy:
        type: "TH1D"
        filename: "spectrum.root"
        nameInFile: "gammas"
        range: [10 keV, 3 MeV]
    - use: "Tl208.dat"

Ions in a box of gas, with more of them near z = 0: 

generator:
  type: "volume"
  shape: "box"
  size: [10 cm, 10 cm, 10 cm]
  spatialDensityFunction: "exp(-abs(z) / 20)"
  source:
    particle: "Pb210"
    angular:
      type: "isotropic"
    energy:
      type: "mono"
      energy: 1 keV

Implementation

TRestGeant4PrimaryGeneratorInfo::LoadDistributions loads the formulas, histograms and Decay0 files and starts CRY once (restG4 master thread) to report any error before the simulation. Each thread has its own TRestGeant4PrimaryGenerator, with its own random generator (seed: metadata seed + thread ID + 1) and copies (Clone) of the spatial generator and of the sources, which load their own distributions, so no locks are needed, except for the CRY generator, shared by the copies and called under a lock (one shower at a time). The GDML positions need the Geant4 geometry: restG4 implements SampleGdmlPosition; everything else does not depend on Geant4. restG4 (PrimaryGeneratorAction) only fills the particle gun.

Differences with legacy

  • The YAML keys are the legacy ones; each section only uses the fields of its type.
  • One cosmic position algorithm for any distribution (legacy had a different one for the cosmic histograms).
  • Removed: back to back (not implemented), the custom and source generator types (not configurable) and the Decay0 seed (the metadata seed is used).
  • The CRY source (TRestGeant4ParticleSourceCry) is the cry generator, with its options in a TRestGeant4CRY section, the whole shower per event and its exposure stored.
  • New: sphere and cylinder volumes, the cone sampled directly, the radius of the cosmic generator, e+ and alpha in Decay0 files (an unknown particle is an error).
  • Fixed: a generator with from has no shape (it used the box one, with size 0: every primary at the volume center), the GDML surfaces (the solid was not set), the bounding box of the GDML volumes (scaled by 1.1, wrong for a box not centered at the origin) and the placement of the GDML volume (see TRestGeant4GeometryInfo::GetPosition).
