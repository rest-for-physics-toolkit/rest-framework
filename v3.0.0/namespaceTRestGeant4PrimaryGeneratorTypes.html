Detailed Description

Named formulas of the primary generator: energy (keV), angle (rad) and energy and angle; the names are case insensitive and an unknown one throws std::invalid_argument. Also the helpers shared by the generator classes (TRestGeant4SpatialGenerator, TRestGeant4EnergyDistribution, TRestGeant4AngularDistribution, TRestGeant4ParticleSource).
