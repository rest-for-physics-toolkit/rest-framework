Detailed Description

The Geant4 physics list of restG4, built from the physics lists of a TRestGeant4PhysicsLists.

Supported lists: G4DecayPhysics, G4RadioactiveDecayPhysics, one electromagnetic list (G4EmLivermorePhysics, G4EmPenelopePhysics, G4EmStandardPhysics_option3 or G4EmStandardPhysics_option4; more than one is an error) and the hadronic G4HadronPhysicsQGSP_BIC_HP, G4IonBinaryCascadePhysics, G4HadronElasticPhysicsHP, G4NeutronTrackingCut and G4EmExtraPhysics. The production cuts and their energy range come from the TRestGeant4PhysicsLists.
