Detailed Description

Recovers the signals of dead channels of a TRestDetectorSignalEvent from their adjacent channels, preserving the energy.

The adjacent channels come from the readout (TRestDetectorReadout::GetAdjacentChannels). For a group of k consecutive dead channels between the alive channels L and R, L and R keep half of their charge and the other half is shared among the dead channels with linear weights: the dead channel j (1 to k, from L) gets (k + 1 - j) / S · L / 2 + j / S · R / 2, S = k (k + 1) / 2, point by point in time. With one dead channel s_i = (s_{i-1} + s_{i+1}) / 2, with two s_i = (2 s_{i-1} + s_{i+2}) / 6 and s_{i+1} = (s_{i-1} + 2 s_{i+2}) / 6, as legacy. A neighbour without signal in the event has no charge; a group is not recovered if neither neighbour has a signal or a neighbour is not in the readout (the edge of a plane). A neighbour shared by two groups gives half of its charge to each one. The signals already present in the dead channels are replaced.

Parameters:

  • channelIds: DAQ IDs of the dead channels.
  • readout: readout (TRestDetectorReadoutManager) that gives the adjacent channels; empty: the data readout.

No observables. Channels that are not in the readout are skipped with a warning.
