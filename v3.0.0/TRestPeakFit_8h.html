std::optional< double > TRestPeakFit::FindPeak (const TH1 &histogram, double start, double end, double smoothing=3, double significance=3)
 Position of the most prominent significant peak of a histogram in [start, end] (the bins with their center inside), robust to noisy spectra: the histogram is smoothed with a gaussian of smoothing bins, and a local maximum is a peak if its prominence (height over the higher of the two minima that separate it from a higher maximum or the edges) is at least significance times its expected fluctuation (square root of its counts). Maxima at the edges of the range are not peaks.

std::pair< double, double > TRestPeakFit::FitLine (TGraph &graph)
 Linear fit (pol1, kept in the graph as "linearFit") of y against x, e.g. the energy against the position of the peaks: (intercept, slope). The points not finite (e.g. the mean of a failed peak fit) are removed with a warning, then points (0, 0) are added if there are less than 2 points. Throws std::runtime_error if no point is left, if all the points have the same x or if the fit fails.
