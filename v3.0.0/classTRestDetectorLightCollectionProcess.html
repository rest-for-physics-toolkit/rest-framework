Detailed Description

Collection of the scintillation photons of the hits (TRestDetectorScintillationProcess) by the readout channels and their detection: one hit per photoelectron, with its time (scintillation profile of the particle, transport and re-emission in the fibres), for the response of the photosensor (TRestDetectorPhotosensorProcess) and TRestDetectorHitsToSignalProcess. A model for each kind of scintillator: localCapture (opaque scintillator, the channels near the photons capture them with a probability that depends on the distance, e.g. the fibres of LiquidO) or attenuation (transparent scintillator, the photons reach the readout of their channel attenuated and delayed with the distance, e.g. a veto with its PMT).

The parameters (YAML keys), the observables and an example are described in the class documentation (TRestDetectorLightCollectionProcess.cxx).

Collection of the scintillation photons of the hits of a TRestDetectorHitsEvent (energies in photons, from TRestDetectorScintillationProcess) by the readout channels and their detection by the photosensor of each channel (SiPM, PMT): the photons reaching it give Binomial(N, quantumEfficiency) photoelectrons. Each photoelectron is an output hit, at a position that the readout assigns to its channel (so TRestDetectorHitsToSignalProcess puts it in that channel), with the particle of the input hit, the charge 1 (one photoelectron) and the time its photon reaches the photosensor 

t = t_hit + t_transport + t_scintillation + t_wls
  • t_transport: the delay to the photosensor of the model (below);
  • t_scintillation: emission time of the photon, sampled from the scintillation profile of the particle of the hit (TRestDetectorLightResponse::GetScintillationTime of the medium with light; the default profile for unknown particles, 0 without profiles or without such a medium);
  • t_wls: re-emission delay of the fibres, Exp(decayTime) of the wls response of wlsMedium.

TRestDetectorPhotosensorProcess then gives the response of the photosensor (time jitter, gain spread, dark counts, crosstalk, afterpulses, saturation) and TRestDetectorHitsToSignalProcess the signals in photoelectrons (its sampling bins the times); the pulse of a photoelectron and the mean gain of the photosensor go into TRestDetectorSignalShapingProcess (e.g. exponential or gaus with a shapingTime of a few ns for a PMT), so the pulse of an event is the scintillation profile convolved with the single photoelectron response, and the electronics into TRestDetectorSignalToRawSignalProcess. The way the light reaches the channels depends on the scintillator (model):

  • localCapture, opaque scintillator (e.g. LiquidO: the light stays near where it is produced and the fibres around it capture it): the channels closer than maxDistance to the hit (TRestDetectorReadout::GetChannelsInRange, e.g. the fibres of a TRestFibreReadout, in the plane transverse to them) capture each photon with probability captureProbability(r), r the distance to the channel; the photons of the hit are distributed among them with a multinomial (a photon is captured by one channel at most; the probabilities of the channels of a position must add up to 1 at most). captureProbability includes the transport in the medium and the trapping of the channel towards its photosensor (for a fibre read at both ends, each end is a channel with its own probability): a ROOT formula of x = r in mm (e.g. a Gaussian light ball) or a graph or histogram of a ROOT file ("file.root:name", e.g. from an optical simulation of Geant4). With wlsMedium (the medium of the fibres with a wls response), captureProbability is the capture by the fibre and the photons are guided half in each direction along it (TRestDetectorReadout::GetGuidedLightPaths of the channel): a fibre read at both ends gives half to each channel; read at one end, half go directly to it, a distance d, and half to the other end, where a fraction endReflectivity of them is reflected back, a distance 2 L - d (L the length of the fibre). Of the photons on a path of length d, Binomial(N, R exp(-d / attenuationLength)) arrive (R = 1 for the direct path), at t + d / lightSpeed. Without wlsMedium the captured photons arrive at once (the transport in the fibre is then part of captureProbability, per channel). The output hits are at the position given by the readout (for a fibre, its read end); their time is that of the hit plus the delay in the fibre.
  • attenuation, transparent scintillator read at one end (e.g. a veto with a light guide and a PMT): all the light goes to the channel of the hit (TRestDetectorReadout::GetChannelFromPosition), the photons reaching its readout are Binomial(N, exp(-d / attenuationLength)) and they arrive at t + d / effectiveLightSpeed, with d the distance to the readout of the channel (TRestDetectorReadout::GetDistanceToReadout, e.g. to the readoutVolume of a TRestGDMLReadout) and the light response of the medium (attenuationLength 0: no attenuation; effectiveLightSpeed 0: no delay). A channel without distance to its readout is an error. The output hits are at the position of the hit.

Hits without any channel collecting their light are counted in the observable NHitsWithoutChannel (their photons are lost); NPhotoelectrons is the total of the event. Events without photoelectrons are rejected.

Parameters: model (localCapture), readout (empty: the data readout of the TRestDetectorReadoutManager), medium (name of the TRestDetectorMedium with light; empty: the only one with light, needed for attenuation), captureProbability, maxDistance and wlsMedium (localCapture), quantumEfficiency (1), seed (0: random seed; otherwise the generator is re-seeded at each event from the seed and the event and subevent IDs).

Differences with the previous version of restv2: the times and charges of the photoelectrons were given by TRestDetectorLightToSignalProcess (removed), now TRestDetectorHitsToSignalProcess as for the charge; with wlsMedium captureProbability was per channel and all the captured light went to its read end, now it is per fibre, half in each direction (the reflected half needs endReflectivity); timeJitter and gainSpread were parameters of this process, now of TRestDetectorPhotosensorProcess.

Examples:

LiquidO (TRestFibreReadout): Gaussian light ball of 10 mm, SiPMs

lightCollection: class: "TRestDetectorLightCollectionProcess" model: "localCapture" captureProbability: "0.03*exp(-x*x/(2*10*10))" maxDistance: 30 mm wlsMedium: "Y11" # medium of the fibres with a wls response quantumEfficiency: 0.4 # SiPMs observables: ["NPhotoelectrons", "NHitsWithoutChannel"] photosensor: class: "TRestDetectorPhotosensorProcess" timeJitter: 0.2 ns gainSpread: 0.1 hitsToSignal: class: "TRestDetectorHitsToSignalProcess" sampling: 0.04 us

Vetoes (TRestGDMLReadout with readoutVolume), medium with attenuationLength and effectiveLightSpeed

lightCollection: class: "TRestDetectorLightCollectionProcess" model: "attenuation" readout: "Veto_Readout" medium: "BC408" quantumEfficiency: 0.25 # PMT

The attenuation model replaces the legacy TRestDetectorLightAttenuationProcess (energy E exp(-d/λ) of the veto hits), now with photons; its parameters (vetoLightAttenuationLength, vetoEffectiveLightSpeed) are the light response of the medium and the distance is to the readout volume of the channel.
