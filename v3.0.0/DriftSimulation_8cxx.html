Computes the drift response of a configuration (see TRestDetectorDriftResponse): the field map (LoadCSTFieldMap) with the Garfield gas of the transport table at the pressure of the medium (CreateGasFromTable), the drift region cut at the boundary plane (the readout normal must be along x, y or z), and electrons electrons drifted with AvalancheMC from each node of the grid. An electron arrives if it ends on the boundary plane; its displacement from the node projected on that plane (along PlaneAxes of the readout normal) and its drift time fill the histograms of the node. The electrons of a node on or below the boundary plane all arrive at once, without displacement (their drift is the amplification's); a node outside the field map has no arrivals. Fills the response (nodes, medium, pressure, temperature and transport table); nothing is written.

Parameters
mediumGas medium of the response (its pressure and temperature).
tableTransport table of the medium (e.g. read from its medium file).
readoutReadout of the detector: distance to the readout and normal.
responseConfiguration of the response, filled.
verbosePrint the progress.
Exceptions
std::runtime_errorif the field map cannot be loaded, the readout has no distance or its normal is not along an axis, or the field map is not above the boundary plane.
