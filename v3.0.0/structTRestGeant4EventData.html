Detailed Description

Flat (structure of arrays) storage of a TRestGeant4Event, one TTree branch per member.

Positions (mm) and directions as their X, Y and Z components (floats), names as the IDs of the TRestGeant4Metadata tables: particles (PDG encoding, TRestGeant4PhysicsInfo), processes (TRestGeant4PhysicsInfo) and volumes (TRestGeant4GeometryInfo). Energies in keV, times in us. The track vectors have one entry per track; the hit vectors (and hitsStorage) one per hit, the hits of the track n being [trackStartIndices[n], trackStartIndices[n] + trackNHits[n]).
