Detailed Description

Digitizes a TRestDetectorSignalEvent into a TRestRawSignalEvent: nPoints samples of width sampling from a trigger time, converted to ADC units (the inverse of TRestRawToDetectorSignalProcess).

Author
Javier Galán javie.nosp@m.r.ga.nosp@m.lan@u.nosp@m.niza.nosp@m.r.es
JuanAn García juana.nosp@m.ngp@.nosp@m.uniza.nosp@m.r.es

Transforms a TRestDetectorSignalEvent into a TRestRawSignalEvent: each signal becomes nPoints samples of width sampling, the first one starting at trigger time - triggerDelay, with the ADC value offset + gain * charge rounded and clipped to [-32768, maxValue] (ADC saturation, counted in the observable NSaturatedSignals). All the signals of the event are digitized, also those without points in the acquisition window (flat at offset).

The charge of each sample depends on the mode:

  • integrate: the charge of the points inside the sample [t, t + sampling) is added (signals made of charges, e.g. from TRestDetectorHitsToSignalProcess);
  • sample: the signal is linearly interpolated at the start of the sample, 0 outside the signal (waveforms, e.g. from TRestDetectorSignalShapingProcess, evaluated on a finer grid).

Trigger time (triggerMode), observable TriggerTime:

  • firstDeposit: time of the first point with positive charge of the event;
  • integralThreshold: first time at which the charge of the event in the previous integralWindow (by default half the acquisition window, as legacy) reaches integralThreshold. Events that do not reach it are rejected;
  • fixed: triggerFixedStartTime;
  • observable: the value of the branch triggerObservable of the analysis tree (an observable of the input file or of a previous process, a scalar; events with a non finite value are rejected). E.g. the vetoes, simulated in their own run on the restG4 file (without the Micromegas signals), with the time of the first deposit in the gas of TRestGeant4AnalysisProcess, triggerObservable: "g4Ana_gas_FirstHitTime", as the common trigger of the DAQ; the drift time of the first electrons and the delay of the veto electronics go into triggerDelay (measured in data). Example libraries/connectors/examples/Geant4ToRawVeto.yaml.

Instead of gain and offset, a linear calibration can be given: the energies calibrationEnergy go to the fractions calibrationRange of the ADC range (0: -32768, 1: 32767). Events without signals are rejected. The noise and the shaping are not done here: use TRestDetectorSignalShapingProcess before (in physical units) and TRestRawSignalAddNoiseProcess after, as for the data.

Differences with legacy: no shaping or noise inside the process, no parameters per channel type and no ...TPC trigger modes (each detector goes in its own pipeline), observable trigger with triggerObservable (legacy triggerModeObservableName) read from the analysis tree, triggerDelay in time units instead of samples, the sample mode, the saturation maxValue and the calibration offset (legacy multiplied the gain by the ADC range twice).

Parameters: sampling (mandatory), nPoints (512), mode (integrate), triggerMode (firstDeposit), triggerObservable (for observable), triggerDelay (0), integralThreshold (0), integralWindow (0), triggerFixedStartTime (0), gain (1), offset (0), calibrationEnergy and calibrationRange ([0, 0]: not used), maxValue (32767).

Example:

signalToRaw: class: "TRestDetectorSignalToRawSignalProcess" sampling: 0.04 us nPoints: 512 mode: "sample" triggerMode: "firstDeposit" triggerDelay: 4 us gain: 10 offset: 250 maxValue: 4095 observables:

  • "TriggerTime"
