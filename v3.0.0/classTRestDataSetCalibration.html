Detailed Description

Energy calibration of a dataset: fits the peaks of known energy in the spectrum of an observable of the reference and adds the calibrated energy, a linear function of the observable. See the class documentation in TRestDataSetCalibration.cxx.

Author
JuanAn García juana.nosp@m.ngp@.nosp@m.uniza.nosp@m.r.es

Energy calibration of a dataset, a process of TRestDataSetProcessManager. The spectrum of observable in the reference (referenceFileName with the cuts, see TRestDataSetProcess) is built, the peaks of known energy are fitted with gaussians and the energy against their position is fitted with a line; the column <name>_<calibratedObservable> (e.g. calib_Energy) = slope * observable + intercept (keV) is added to the dataset.

The position of the peaks is estimated from the maximum of the spectrum, which should be the first peak: each peak is fitted in ±window (15%) of its estimated position, or in its range if it is given. The fits are made with TRestPeakFit (RooFit): a gaussian (as legacy) or a Crystal Ball, optionally with a constant or linear background under the peak. A peak whose fit gives no finite position is not used for the linear fit (with a warning); it throws if no peak is left or the linear fit fails.

Parameters

  • observable: the observable calibrated.
  • calibratedObservable: name of the calibrated observable, the column <name>_<calibratedObservable> ("Energy": calib_Energy for a process named calib, as legacy).
  • peaks: list of peaks, energy (keV by default, e.g. "5.9 keV") and optionally range (of the observable where it is fitted, e.g. [9000, 13000]). The first one should be the maximum of the spectrum.
  • nBins: number of bins of the spectrum (1024).
  • range: range of the observable of the spectrum, e.g. [0, 20000]; automatic if not given.
  • zeroPoint: add the point (0, 0) to the linear fit (true, as legacy).
  • peakModel: "gaussian" (default, as legacy) or "crystalBall" (low energy tail).
  • background: "none" (default, as legacy), "constant" or "linear" under each peak.
  • window: half width of the fit window of the peaks without range, relative to their estimated position (0.15, as legacy).
  • peakSearch: search each peak in its range (or its estimated window) and fit it in ±window around the peak found, inside the range (false). The search (TRestPeakFit::FindPeak) only accepts significant peaks (prominence over 3 times the statistical fluctuation, smoothed spectrum): if there is none, the peak is fitted in the whole range.
  • referenceFileName, cuts: see TRestDataSetProcess.

Results

  • slope, intercept: of the calibration, energy = slope * observable + intercept.

  • peaks: of each peak, position (mean of the fit), fwhm (of its gaussian core, relative to the mean, %), fitRange (where it was fitted), fitStatus (0: converged) and fitParameters, the free parameters of the fit as name: [value, error], which depend on peakModel and background:

    • mean, sigma: of the gaussian (core), in units of the observable;
    • alpha, n (crystalBall): the tail starts at alpha sigmas below the mean and falls with power n;
    • signalCounts: counts of the peak (in the fit range);
    • backgroundCounts (constant or linear background): counts of the background in the fit range;
    • backgroundSlope (linear background): relative slope, from -1 to 1 over the fit range.

    E.g. {energy: 5.9, position: 5021, fwhm: 18.2, fitRange: [4300, 5800], fitStatus: 0, fitParameters: {mean: [5021, 1.3], sigma: [389, 1.1], signalCounts: [102000, 330]}}.

  • referenceUsed, parametersFileName: see TRestDataSetProcess.

Objects (folder RESTDataSetStore/TRestDataSetCalibration/<name> of the output dataset): spectrum (with the peak fits g0, g1..., drawn with it) and linearFit (energy against position, with the linear fit).

Example (in a TRestDataSetProcessManager):

calib: class: "TRestDataSetCalibration" referenceFileName: "Dataset_Calibration.root" observable: "trackAnalysis_MaxTrackEnergy" nBins: 1024 range: [0, 20000] peaks:

  • {energy: "5.9 keV"}

Differences with legacy (TRestDataSetCalibration): a process of a dataset pipeline (no Calibrate nor dataSetName/outputFileName); calibFile replaced by referenceFileName (a dataset processed by this calibration reuses its parameters, otherwise it is fitted); the column is <name>_<calibratedObservable> (legacy calib_Energy, fixed); peaks with an optional fit range and the fit results; zeroPoint can be disabled; the peaks are fitted with RooFit (TRestPeakFit, binned likelihood) instead of TH1::Fit, optionally with a Crystal Ball and a background, in a configurable window; the FWHM factor is 2.355 (legacy 2.35).
