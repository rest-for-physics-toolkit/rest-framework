Detailed Description

Top section of a restGarfield configuration (as TRestGeant4Manager for restG4): a medium (TRestDetectorMedium, the medium key) and the tables to compute for it, added to its mediumFile: transport (TRestDetectorTransportTable) and gain (TRestDetectorGainTable), keys without class, each one a table configuration or a list of them. restGarfield computes the transport tables first, then the gain tables, then the drift responses in field maps (drift, TRestDetectorDriftResponse, each one in its own file, with the transport table of the medium file), then the amplification responses in the field maps of a unit cell (amplification, TRestDetectorAmplificationResponse, each one in its own file). The classes do not depend on Garfield++.

transportManager: class: "TRestDetectorTransportManager" medium: class: "TRestDetectorMedium" name: "ArIso" components: {Ar: 99, iC4H10: 1} transport: fieldRange: [10 V/cm, 1000 V/cm] gain: pressures: [1 bar, 1.4 bar] gap: 128 um reducedFieldRange: [25 kV/cm/bar, 35 kV/cm/bar]
