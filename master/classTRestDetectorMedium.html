Detailed Description

A material (gas, liquid or solid) at its conditions, with its medium file (the tables generated by restGarfield, read without Garfield++) and its responses. It describes the material, not the detector technology: what is measured in it is given by the readout and the responses.

The same section is used by restGarfield (the medium of a TRestDetectorTransportManager: it writes its tables to mediumFile, components needed) and by the processes (a metadata of the TRestManager, found by name with their medium field). It is self-contained: LoadConfig reads the medium file, if it exists, and gives it with the conditions to its responses.

Parameters:

  • name: the name of the medium in its file (default: the name of the section, as any metadata).
  • state: gas, liquid or solid.
  • components: names and molar fractions in percent (for gases, volume fractions or partial pressures), e.g. {Ar: 99, iC4H10: 1} or {Si: 100}. Read from the medium file if not given; if given, they must agree with it.
  • mediumFile: path of the medium file (a leading ~ is $HOME). restGarfield writes to it (by default <name>.root). It can be a URL for reading (downloaded and cached, TRestTools::GetLocalFile; e.g. a versioned table of rest-common); a URL that cannot be downloaded is an error.
  • pressure (gases only, 1 bar), temperature (293.15 K): the conditions of the detector (and the default ones of the tables of restGarfield). A pressure other than 1 bar for a liquid or a solid is an error (1 bar is accepted, it is written to the metadata for any state).

  • Responses, each one a key without class: charge (TRestDetectorChargeResponse: ionisation, transport and gain), light (TRestDetectorLightResponse: production and propagation of the scintillation light) and wls (TRestDetectorWLSResponse: wavelength shifting, re-emission and guiding of the light).

    medium: class: "TRestDetectorMedium" name: "ArIso" mediumFile: "~/.rest/media/ArIso.root" pressure: 1.4 bar charge: wValue: 26.2 eV fanoFactor: 0.2 driftField: 140 V/cm
