Detailed Description

Event container holding the signals (time, amplitude points) of the readout channels of one event, in physical units and double precision (unlike the digitized TRestRawSignalEvent).

Each signal is identified by its DAQ channel ID (one signal per ID) and accessed through a TRestDetectorSignal view (GetSignal, GetSignalByID). Branches of the event tree: fSigTime, fSigAmplitude, fSigIDs and fSigOffsets (plus those of TRestEvent). Produced by TRestDetectorHitsToSignalProcess (simulations) or from the raw signals (TRestRawToDetectorSignalProcess, connectors library), and used by the signal processes of this library (shaping, recovery, peaks finder, signal to hits) and by TRestDetectorSignalToRawSignalProcess (connectors).
