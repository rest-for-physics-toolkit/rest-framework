Detailed Description

This namespace defines generic functions to fit peaks of spectra (histograms) and the linear calibration of their positions, e.g. for the energy calibrations (TRestDataSetCalibration, TRestDataSetGainMap).

The peaks are fitted with RooFit (binned maximum likelihood in the fit range, the bins inside it): a gaussian or a Crystal Ball (gaussian with a power-law tail on the low side, e.g. incomplete charge collection), plus optionally a constant or linear background under the peak. The fit is kept in the histogram (its list of functions, so it is written, read and drawn with it) as a TF1 name with the parameters Constant, Mean and Sigma (Alpha and N for the Crystal Ball, Background and Slope for the background), in the histogram scale. The RooFitResult is not kept: written to a file it adds a TProcessID (ProcessID0, for the references of the RooFit variables); its free parameters are in Peak::parameters.
