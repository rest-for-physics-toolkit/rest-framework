Detailed Description

Event viewer (for TRestBrowser) of the files analysed with TRestTrackLineAnalysisProcess, for the event type selected in the browser:

  • TRestTrackEvent and TRestDetectorHitsEvent: the event (its DrawEvent with the plot option) with dashed lines from the origin (open circle) to the end (star) over every view where they are defined:
    • black, the reconstructed line: observables OriginX/Y/Z and EndX/Y/Z of the entry (branches processName_OriginX, ...: of the TRestTrackLineAnalysisProcess given, or if none, of the first process that has them);
    • red, the Geant4 origin and end of simulated files: observables EntryX/Y/Z and EndX/Y/Z of a volume of TRestGeant4AnalysisProcess (branches processName_volume_EntryX, ...: of the prefix given, e.g. "g4Ana_gas", or if none, of the first one that has them), where the first particle enters or is created in the volume and where it ends. The Z of the hits is not comparable with the Geant4 one (on the readout plane after the drift, or reconstructed from the times with its origin at the trigger), so only the XY view gets the line; the views with Z get dotted lines at the X or Y of the origin and end (markers at their top) and the 3D view nothing. Without them only the event is drawn. With an empty plot option, the hits events are drawn with "markers(colz)": Z bins of the pulse samples and the 3D view as markers colored by energy (TRestDetectorHitsEvent::DrawEvent).
  • TRestRawSignalEvent and TRestDetectorSignalEvent: with an empty plot option, the signals in one pad per readout axis: X strips (channels with X and no Y), Y strips (Y and no X) and pixels (X and Y), from the readout of the file (TRestDetectorReadoutManager, the first readout if there are several); channels not in the readout are not drawn. With a plot option, or without readout, the event DrawEvent.
  • Other events: as TRestEventViewer.
