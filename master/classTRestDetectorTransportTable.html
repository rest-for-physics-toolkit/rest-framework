Detailed Description

Charge transport of a medium versus the electric field: the configuration of its tables (the transport key of a TRestDetectorTransportManager, computed by restGarfield) and one table (one condition), read from or written to the medium file without Garfield++.

Configuration: one table per temperature at a single pressure (1 bar by default) over the field grid, added to the medium file (the mediumFile of the TRestDetectorMedium). The transport of a gas depends on E/p, so a table at one pressure is scaled to the others (see below): tables at more pressures are not needed (REST_Garfield_DrawMedium with a pressure compares the scaled tables, to check it). Tables that already exist (same key: pressure, temperature and field range) are skipped unless overwrite is set. temperatures, if empty, are that of the medium; the pressure only applies to gases (other media keep it for the key); nCollisions and maxElectronEnergy are Magboltz parameters, only used for gases.

usePenning (gases, false by default) applies the Penning transfer to the Townsend coefficient with the transfer probability penningRate, as Garfield++ EnablePenningTransfer: the excitations above the ionisation potential of another component ionise with that probability. It only changes the Townsend coefficient, so drift tables do not need it. Penning tables have their own key (suffix _Penning<rate>) and coexist with the tables without Penning.

transport: pressure: 1 bar temperatures: [293.15] # K fieldRange: [10 V/cm, 1000 V/cm] fieldNodes: 20 logField: true nCollisions: 10 maxElectronEnergy: 400 eV

Medium file, e.g. ArIso.root (it can have several media, e.g. Ar99_iC4H10_1 and Ar98_iC4H10_2, concentrations of a mixture):

  • RESTMetadataStore/<medium>: YAML (TObjString, as the other REST metadata) with the medium (name, state, components) and tables, the list of its transport tables: key, pressure, temperature, fieldRange, pressureScaling, penningRate (0 without Penning), carriers and generator, the configuration that computed it. Adding a table adds (or replaces) its entry.
  • tables/<medium>/<key>/: the TGraphs <medium>_<key>_<carrier>_<quantity> versus the field in V/cm, with self-contained titles, for each carrier (electron, hole, ion): driftVelocity (mm/us), longitudinalDiffusion and transversalDiffusion (sqrt(cm)), townsend (impact ionisation) and attachment (attachment or trapping) (1/cm).

The key is the condition and field range, e.g. 1bar_293.15K_E10-1000Vcm (or 1bar_293.15K_E1000-100000Vcm_Penning0.3). Gas tables (pressureScaling) are scaled to other pressures as Garfield++ does: the drift velocity depends on E/p, the diffusion coefficients scale as 1/sqrt(p) and the Townsend and attachment coefficients as p. The getters take the field in V/mm and the pressure in bar (REST units); values are interpolated linearly in log(E) (in log of the value for Townsend and attachment) and kept constant out of the range.
