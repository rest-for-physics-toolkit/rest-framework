class  TRestLogLine
 One message, written when the statement ends (RESTInfo << ... << RESTendl;) if its level is enabled by the verbose of the context. Each statement has its own buffer, so messages of different threads are not mixed. The similar messages (at most 10 different characters) of RESTWarning and RESTInfo (file not nullptr) repeated from the same call site (file and line) are suppressed after TRestLogManager::maxRepeated, unless the verbose of the context is debug.
