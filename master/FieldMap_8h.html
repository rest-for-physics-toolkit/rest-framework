Loads the electric field exported by CST Studio Suite as ASCII (2D/3D field result, ASCIIExport with file type "ascii", a regular grid: Mode "FixedWidth" or "FixedNumber") into a Garfield++ ComponentGrid, without potential.

The file has a header line with the columns and their units, e.g. x [mm] y [mm] z [mm] Ex [V/m] Ey [V/m] Ez [V/m], then a line of dashes and one line per node x y z Ex Ey Ez (CST 2026). The units of the header (any length and field units of REST_Units, e.g. mm, um, m and V/m, V/cm) convert the values to the Garfield units (cm, V/cm); the grid is deduced from the nodes (ComponentGrid "XYZ"). The coordinates are moved by offset (mm), the position in the detector frame of the origin of the CST frame. The nodes with the field exactly 0 (Ex = Ey = Ez = 0) are out of the gas: CST writes 0 out of the solved volume (e.g. the corners of the exported box around a cylindrical gas volume) and inside the conductors. They are inactive nodes of the grid: in a cell with one of them, ComponentGrid gives no medium (status -5) and a drift or an avalanche ends there, like at a wall. The Garfield medium of the grid is not set (ComponentGrid::SetMedium).

The dielectrics (e.g. the kapton of a microbulk Micromegas) have a field and are not distinguished from the gas by it: dielectricFile is the export of the electric displacement D on nodes of the same grid (header x [um] ... Dx [C/m^2] ...; all of them or a part, e.g. only the layers with dielectrics). A node where |D| > 1.5 eps0 |E| (relative permittivity above 1.5; 1 in the gas) is inactive as well: the electrons that reach a dielectric are lost (its charging up is not simulated).
