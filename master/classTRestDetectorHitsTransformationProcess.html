Detailed Description

Scales the energy of the hits of a TRestDetectorHitsEvent and mirrors, rotates and translates their positions, in this order (replaces the legacy TRestDetectorHitsNormalizationProcess, TRestDetectorHitsSpecularProcess, TRestDetectorHitsRotationProcess and TRestDetectorHitsTranslationProcess).

The parameters (YAML keys), the observables and an example are described in the class documentation (TRestDetectorHitsTransformationProcess.cxx).

Transforms the hits of a TRestDetectorHitsEvent. The operations are applied in this order, each one only if it is given:

  1. energyFactor: multiplies the energy of all the hits (legacy TRestDetectorHitsNormalizationProcess).
  2. mirrorPosition, mirrorNormal: reflects the positions on the plane through mirrorPosition with normal mirrorNormal (legacy TRestDetectorHitsSpecularProcess). A normal (0, 0, 0) gives no mirror.
  3. rotationCenter, rotationAxis, rotationAngle: rotates the positions by rotationAngle (right hand) around the axis through rotationCenter along rotationAxis (legacy TRestDetectorHitsRotationProcess).
  4. translation: adds the vector to the positions (legacy TRestDetectorHitsTranslationProcess).

As legacy, the positions of the hits that are not XYZ (e.g. veto or strip hits, with undefined coordinates) are not changed; their energy is scaled.

Parameters: energyFactor (1), mirrorPosition ((0, 0, 0) mm), mirrorNormal ((0, 0, 0)), rotationCenter ((0, 0, 0) mm), rotationAxis ((0, 0, 1)), rotationAngle (0 deg), translation ((0, 0, 0) mm).

Example (detector rotated 90 degrees around z and moved 10 cm along x):

hitsTransformation: class: "TRestDetectorHitsTransformationProcess" rotationAxis: [0, 0, 1] rotationAngle: 90 deg translation: [100, 0, 0]

Differences with legacy: one process instead of four; the legacy defaults (translation (0, 0, 1) mm, specular plane z = 1 mm with normal z) are replaced by no transformation; the mirror is not applied to the non-XYZ hits either (legacy specular did the same).
