std::vector< std::vector< size_t > > TRestHitsAnalysis::FindClusters (const std::vector< XYZVector > &points, double distance, const std::array< bool, 3 > &coordinates={true, true, true})
 Groups the points in clusters: two points closer than distance belong to the same cluster, and so do the points connected through a chain of such pairs. The distance only uses the coordinates enabled in coordinates (e.g. {true, false, true} for XZ hits), the others are ignored (they can be NaN, as in the hit positions). The points are placed in cells of side distance, so each point is only compared with those of its cell and the adjacent ones (time growing with the number of points, not with its square).
 
std::vector< size_t > TRestHitsAnalysis::GetShortestPath (const std::vector< XYZVector > &points, bool cyclic=false, size_t maxExactPoints=12)
 Order of the points along the shortest path through all of them (travelling salesman), open (from any point to any other) or, with cyclic, closed (back to the first point). Exact (Held-Karp dynamic programming) up to maxExactPoints points (at most 16, time and memory grow as 2^n); with more points, nearest neighbour paths (from every point, or from the farthest point from the centroid with more than 200 points) improved with 2-opt (reversing segments while the path gets shorter), which is not always the shortest one.
 
std::vector< Node > TRestHitsAnalysis::KMeansClustering (const std::vector< XYZVector > &points, const std::vector< double > &energies, const std::vector< XYZVector > &centroids, int maxIt=100, bool fixBoundaries=false)
 Reduces the points to nodes with k-means, starting from the given nodes (centroids), as legacy TRestVolumeHits::kMeansClustering: each node is the energy weighted mean of the points closest to it, its energy their sum and its sigma their energy weighted spread. With fixBoundaries, the first and last nodes stay where they are, with no points, energy nor sigma.
 
std::vector< Node > TRestHitsAnalysis::MergePoints (const std::vector< XYZVector > &points, const std::vector< double > &energies, double startingDistance, double minimumDistance, double distanceStepFactor, size_t maxNodes, const std::vector< XYZVector > &sigmas={})
 Reduces the points to nodes merging the close ones, as legacy TRestTrackReductionProcess: the pairs of nodes closer than a distance are merged (energy weighted position, summed energy) until there are none, starting with startingDistance and multiplying it by distanceStepFactor while it is below minimumDistance or there are more than maxNodes nodes (at least 1). The sigma of each node is the energy weighted spread of its points, including their own sigmas (if given, same order as the points), e.g. for points that are already nodes.
 
bool TRestHitsAnalysis::GetLine (const std::vector< XYZVector > &points, const std::vector< double > &energies, XYZVector &first, XYZVector &last)
 Segment of the line that best fits the points: through their energy weighted mean, along the direction of largest spread (principal axis of the energy weighted covariance, i.e. the line minimizing the squared distances to it), between the extreme projections of the points on it. Works for 1D, 2D and 3D points: the coordinates the points do not define must have the same value in all of them (TRestHits::GetPoints sets them to 0), the line then stays in the projection they define.

double TRestHitsAnalysis::GetTrackEnds (const std::vector< XYZVector > &points, const std::vector< double > &energies, XYZVector &origin, XYZVector &end)
 Origin and end of a track given by its points in order: the end is the extreme (first or last point) on the half of the track with more energy (the Bragg peak), the origin the other one. The halves split the projections of the points on the segment from the first to the last point (the point order for a single point or coinciding extremes). Using the energy of each half instead of the most energetic point (legacy) keeps a single noisy point from reversing the track.
