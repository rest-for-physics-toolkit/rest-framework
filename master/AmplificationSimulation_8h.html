Computes the amplification response of a configuration (see TRestDetectorAmplificationResponse): the field maps of the cell (LoadCSTFieldMaps, with their dielectric maps and the periodicity) with the Garfield gas of the composition of the medium at its pressure and temperature (CreateGas, with the Penning transfer if usePenning), and electrons avalanches (AvalancheMicroscopic) each started by one electron at a random position over the cell on the plane z = start. The size of an avalanche is its number of electrons ending below z = anode; one with electrons there is collected, with the mean time of those electrons. Fills the response (medium, conditions, counts, histograms, the map of the cell by the start point and the electrons at the anode out of the cell of their primary); nothing is written.

Parameters
mediumGas medium of the response (its components, pressure and temperature).
responseConfiguration of the response, filled.
verbosePrint the progress.
jobsProcesses (fork) sharing the avalanches (Garfield++ is not thread safe), each with its own random seed drawn from the generator of Garfield++; 1: in this process.
Exceptions
std::runtime_errorif the medium is not a gas, a field map cannot be loaded, the start or anode planes are not inside the maps, the Penning transfer cannot be enabled, or a process fails.
