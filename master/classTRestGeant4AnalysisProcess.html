Detailed Description

Observables of a TRestGeant4Event, one value per event but the interactions: global and primary ones and, for each selection, energy (also per particle), mean position, first hit time, tracks and the particle entering its volumes.

The input event is passed unchanged to the output. Configuration keys: selections (a list of TRestGeant4SelectionInfo) and the common process keys (e.g. observables). The registered observables (totalEdep, sensitiveVolumeEnergy, nTracks, boundingSize, energyPrimary, x/y/zOriginPrimary, x/y/zDirectionPrimary, thetaPrimary, phiPrimary, eventPrimaryParticleName, subEventPrimaryParticleName and the <name>_* ones of each selection), the YAML example and the differences with legacy are documented in TRestGeant4AnalysisProcess.cxx (detailed description below).

Observables of a TRestGeant4Event. Energies in keV, positions in mm and angles in rad; times in us, relative to the first energy deposit of the event (TRestGeant4Event::GetFirstDepositTime), the origin of the times of TRestGeant4ToDetectorHitsProcess (e.g. a trigger time from FirstHitTime).

Global and primary observables: totalEdep, sensitiveVolumeEnergy, nTracks, boundingSize (diagonal of the box containing the hits with energy), energyPrimary, x/y/zOriginPrimary, x/y/zDirectionPrimary, thetaPrimary, phiPrimary, eventPrimaryParticleName and subEventPrimaryParticleName ("generator" for the main event) (strings).

Selections: each entry of selections selects the hits with energy of the given volumes (regular expressions, as in TRestGeant4QuenchingProcess), particles and processes (Geant4 names, e.g. compt, phot, eIoni, hadElastic, nCapture); an empty or missing list of particles or processes selects any, e.g. only volumes for all the particles and processes in those volumes. Without volumes, the sensitive volumes of the simulation are selected (volumes: [".*"] or ["^."] for all of them). Without selections, there is one for each sensitive entry of activeVolumes in the simulation, of its volumes, named as its name (characters not allowed as _, without the leading and trailing ones) and with entering: true, e.g. gas_Energy, gas_EntryX... for name: "gas", and veto_* for a second one name: "veto". All the observables are one value per event, but the interaction vectors; each selection gives:

  • <name>Energy, <name>_MeanPosX/Y/Z (energy weighted), <name>_FirstHitTime and <name>_NTracks (tracks with energy in the selection). Without energy, MeanPos and FirstHitTime are NaN.
  • <name><particle>_Energy for each particle of splitByParticle: the energy of the selection given by that particle. In the observable name, - and + become Minus and Plus and any other character not allowed _ (e.g. gas_eMinus_Energy).
  • With entering: true, the particle entering the volumes of the selection: for each track with energy in the selection, the first one of the track and its ancestors whose first hit is out of the volumes, i.e. the particle which entered them giving the energy (e.g. the alpha or the neutron entering the gas). Observables: <name>_NEntering (number of different ones, 1 for a single entering particle) and, of the one with the lowest ID:
    • <name>_EntryParticle, _EntryParentParticle (empty for a primary) and _EntryCreatorProcess (strings);
    • <name>_EntryEnergy, _EntryTime and _EntryX/Y/Z: kinetic energy, time and position of its first hit in the volumes;
    • <name>_EndX/Y/Z: position of its last hit in the volumes (e.g. where the alpha stops in the gas, or where a gamma leaves it), to compare with the reconstructed tracks;
    • <name>_InitialEnergy and _InitialX/Y/Z: kinetic energy and position where it was created. If the energy is given only by particles created in the volumes (e.g. a source inside the gas), <name>_NEntering is 0 and these observables are those of the primary created in the volumes with the lowest ID (the first ancestor of the tracks with energy, e.g. the alpha of the source): the entry is its first hit, where it starts. A primary at rest (an ion that decays, e.g. Po210) is not one: its products are; to take only one of them (e.g. the alpha, not the recoil nucleus or the gammas), a selection with particles: ["alpha"]. A track whose parent is not in the event (removed without hits, registerEmptyTracks: false) is also taken as created in the volumes. Without such a primary, the strings are empty and the numbers NaN. Its cost is a walk over the ancestors of the tracks with energy in the selection.
  • With perVolume: true: <name>_<volume>_Energy, the energy of each volume of the selection (the geometry name with the characters not allowed as _; an error if two volumes give the same one), and <name>_MaxVolumeEnergy, the largest of them (e.g. the most energetic panel of a veto, to apply a threshold per panel). Groups and layers are selections with their own expressions.
  • With interactions: true: the selected hits with or without energy, i.e. the interaction points of the selected particles and processes (meant for discrete processes, e.g. particles: ["neutron"], processes: ["nCapture"] for the neutron captures; without processes every step counts). <name>_NInteractions and, one entry per interaction, the vectors <name>_InteractionX/Y/Z, _InteractionTime, _InteractionKineticEnergy (of the particle at the hit) and _InteractionSecondariesEnergy (initial kinetic energy of the secondaries its track created by that process, e.g. the capture gammas and the recoiling nucleus; if the track has several interactions with that process, each secondary goes to the closest one); _InteractionTrackEdep and _InteractionTrackMaxVolumeEdep: energy deposited by the track of the interaction and all its descendants in trackEdepVolumes (regular expressions, all the volumes if not given), in total and in the volume with the most (e.g. for a captured neutron with the veto volumes: the energy in the vetoes of the neutron, its recoils before the capture and the capture products, to tag it; the same for all the interactions of a track). Only the hits stored by restG4: the volumes must be active volumes, and with removeUnwantedTracks a track without energy in the keep volumes is kept only as an ancestor of a kept track (e.g. the neutron of the capture gammas) or with removeUnwantedTracksKeepZeroEnergyTracks.

At initialization an error is thrown for a volume list without any volume in the geometry, for a particle or process not in the physics information of the simulation metadata, for a splitByParticle particle excluded by particles and for a selection without volumes when there is no sensitive volume; for each event, a selection without hits is just 0 (empty).

Difference with legacy: the observables are not defined by the observable name (<volume>VolumeEDep, <volume>MeanPos<X|Y|Z>, <volume><Process>Process, <particle>TracksEDep, PerProcess<Name>, containsProcess<Process>, <particle>TracksCounter) but by the selections, e.g. containsProcessCompt is compt_Energy > 0, PerProcess<Name> normalized is <name>_Energy / sensitiveVolumeEnergy and <particle>TracksCounter is the <name>_NTracks of a selection by particle (only the tracks with energy). firstTrackInSensitive* are the <name>_Entry* observables of a selection of the sensitive volumes with entering: true (firstTrackInSensitiveOk is <name>_NEntering == 1), with the entry and end points. The number of hits is not an observable, it depends on the Geant4 step length. As legacy, processes sharing Geant4 type and subtype (e.g. eIoni and ionIoni) share the ID, are registered once and are not distinguished. zenithYDegrees and zenithSourceDegrees are removed (they follow from the primary direction).

Also replaces legacy TRestGeant4VetoAnalysisProcess and TRestGeant4NeutronTaggingProcess:

  • Veto energies (EnergyVeto_<alias>, EnergyTotal, EnergyMax; NeutronTagging <volume>VolumeEDep, vetoAllEVetoMax): a selection of the veto volumes with perVolume (<name>_<volume>Energy, <name>_Energy, <name>_MaxVolumeEnergy). Groups and layers (EnergyGroup<g>, EnergyGroupLayer_*, EnergyGroupMax_<g>, EnergyLayerMax_*, vetoGroup<G>EVetoMax), parsed by legacy from the volume names: one selection with perVolume per group or layer, with its own volume expression.
  • Quenched veto energies (NeutronTagging quenchingFactors, one factor for all the particles but e± and gammas): TRestGeant4QuenchingProcess (Birks per volume) before the analysis.
  • Neutron captures (nCaptures..., neutronsCaptured...): a selection with particles: ["neutron"], processes: ["nCapture"] and interactions (positions, times, energy of the capture products), one per volume set (e.g. capture layers and scintillators, legacy captureLayerVolume and scintillatorVolume): NCaptures = <name>_NInteractions; neutronsCapturedEDepByNeutronAndChildren → <name>_InteractionTrackEdep without trackEdepVolumes, ...AndChildrenInVeto and ...InVetoMax → <name>_InteractionTrackEdep and _InteractionTrackMaxVolumeEdep with trackEdepVolumes the veto volumes (another selection for each set of volumes). The secondary neutrons leaving the shielding (secondaryNeutronsShielding...) are a selection with particles: ["neutron"], processes: ["Transportation"], the shielding volumes and interactions (position and kinetic energy; legacy excluded the primaries and counted each track once). Not migrated: the initial energy of the neutron track, the neutron alone (EDepByNeutron, ...InVeto), the minimum per veto and the list of capture gammas (their total energy is _InteractionSecondariesEnergy).

Example:

g4Ana: class: "TRestGeant4AnalysisProcess" observables: ["all"] selections:

  • name: "gas" volumes: ["^gasVolume"] splitByParticle: ["alpha", "e-", "proton"] entering: true
  • name: "gasCompton" volumes: ["^gasVolume"] processes: ["compt"]
  • name: "scint" volumes: ["^scintillatorVolume"] perVolume: true
  • name: "captures" particles: ["neutron"] processes: ["nCapture"] volumes: [".*"] interactions: true trackEdepVolumes: ["^scintillatorVolume"]
