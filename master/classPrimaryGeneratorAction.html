Detailed Description

Fills the particle gun with the primaries sampled by TRestGeant4PrimaryGenerator (one per thread)

The generator is configured by the generator section of the TRestGeant4Metadata (TRestGeant4PrimaryGeneratorInfo); its seed is the metadata seed plus the thread ID plus 1. The positions in GDML volumes are sampled with the Geant4 solid of the generator volume (DetectorConstruction).
