Detailed Description

Response of the photosensors (SiPM, PMT) to the photoelectrons of a TRestDetectorHitsEvent (a hit per photoelectron, TRestDetectorLightCollectionProcess): dark counts, optical crosstalk, afterpulses, saturation of the cells of a SiPM, the time jitter and the gain spread. A hit per avalanche, to be collected by TRestDetectorHitsToSignalProcess.

The parameters (YAML keys), the observables and an example are described in the class documentation (TRestDetectorPhotosensorProcess.cxx).

Author
JuanAn García juana.nosp@m.ngp@.nosp@m.uniza.nosp@m.r.es

Response of the photosensors of the readout channels, SiPMs or PMTs, to the photoelectrons of a TRestDetectorHitsEvent: TRestDetectorLightCollectionProcess gives a hit per photoelectron (charge 1, the time its photon reaches the photosensor), this process gives a hit per avalanche of the photosensor, with the same channel (position), time and particle, adding the effects of the photosensor:

  • dark counts: Poisson(darkCountRate * T) avalanches per channel, uniform in darkCountWindow (of length T), on every channel of the readout (particle 0), at a position of the channel (its read end, or its centre if the read end is not in the channel, e.g. the PMT of a veto);
  • optical crosstalk (SiPM): each avalanche triggers another one at the same time with probability crosstalkProbability, which may trigger another one (geometric number of crosstalk avalanches);
  • afterpulses: each avalanche is followed with probability afterpulseProbability by another one, delayed by Exp(afterpulseDelay) (SiPM, trapped carriers) or by Gauss(afterpulseDelay, afterpulseSpread) (PMT, ions); afterpulses and crosstalk avalanches may give crosstalk and afterpulses in turn;
  • saturation (SiPM): each avalanche fires a random cell of the cells of its channel; an avalanche on a cell that fired less than cellRecoveryTime before (0: at any time of the event) is lost. For n simultaneous photoelectrons the number of avalanches is cells * (1 - exp(-n / cells)) on average;
  • the time jitter of the photosensor, Gaussian of timeJitter (transit time spread), and the charge of an avalanche, 1 or Gauss(1, gainSpread) truncated at 0 (gain fluctuation, e.g. 0.3-0.4 for a PMT, 0.1 for a SiPM), in units of the mean single photoelectron charge.

The avalanches of a channel are processed in time order, from the input photoelectrons (an input hit of charge q is round(q) photoelectrons) and the dark counts. Hits without a channel (TRestDetectorReadout::GetChannelFromPosition) are dropped. The effects not wanted are left at 0 (all 0: one hit of charge 1 per photoelectron, unchanged). The quantum efficiency is that of TRestDetectorLightCollectionProcess (it decides which photons give photoelectrons); the pulse of an avalanche and the mean gain go into TRestDetectorSignalShapingProcess. The saturation of the charge of a PMT (non-linearity at high currents) is not modelled.

Observables: NAvalanches (output hits), NDarkCounts, NCrosstalk, NAfterpulses (avalanches of each kind in the output) and NSaturationLosses (avalanches lost on busy cells).

Parameters: readout (empty: the data readout of the TRestDetectorReadoutManager), timeJitter (0), gainSpread (0), darkCountRate (0, per channel, e.g. "100 kHz"), darkCountWindow (needed with darkCountRate), crosstalkProbability (0), afterpulseProbability (0), afterpulseDelay, afterpulseSpread (0: exponential delay), cells (0: no saturation), cellRecoveryTime (0), seed (0: random seed; otherwise the generator is re-seeded at each event from the seed and the event and subevent IDs). crosstalkProbability + afterpulseProbability must be below 1 (a finite number of avalanches).

Examples:

SiPMs (LiquidO fibres)

photosensor: class: "TRestDetectorPhotosensorProcess" timeJitter: 0.2 ns gainSpread: 0.1 darkCountRate: 100 kHz darkCountWindow: [-4 us, 16 us] crosstalkProbability: 0.05 afterpulseProbability: 0.02 afterpulseDelay: 50 ns cells: 3600 cellRecoveryTime: 50 ns observables: ["NAvalanches", "NDarkCounts", "NCrosstalk", "NAfterpulses", "NSaturationLosses"]

PMT (vetoes): ion afterpulses about 1.5 us after the pulse

photosensor: class: "TRestDetectorPhotosensorProcess" readout: "Veto_Readout" timeJitter: 0.5 ns gainSpread: 0.35 darkCountRate: 1 kHz darkCountWindow: [-4 us, 16 us] afterpulseProbability: 0.01 afterpulseDelay: 1.5 us afterpulseSpread: 0.2 us

The time jitter and the gain spread were parameters of TRestDetectorLightCollectionProcess in the previous version of restv2 (timeJitter, gainSpread).
