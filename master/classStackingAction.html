Detailed Description

Classifies the new tracks and splits the radioactive decays in sub-events.

Neutrinos are killed. The products of the decay of an unstable nucleus whose lifetime is above the subEventTimeDelay of the metadata are delayed to a new sub-event (full chain) or killed (no full chain). The isotopes of the full chain stop list are killed, and the secondaries created after the crossing point of a step entering a biasing volume (OutputManager::ApplyBiasingVolume).
