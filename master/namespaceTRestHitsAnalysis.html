Detailed Description

This namespace defines generic functions to analyse sets of hits (or track nodes), given as positions and, when needed, their energies (same order), so they can be used with any hits container (e.g. TRestHits::GetPoints and TRestHits::GetEnergies).

2D (or 1D) hits: the distances are computed with the three coordinates, so the undefined ones must have the same value in all the points (TRestHits::GetPoints sets them to 0), which gives the distance in the projection the hits define. FindClusters instead takes the coordinates to use.

double TRestHitsAnalysis::GetGaussSigma ( const std::vector< double > &  values,
const std::vector< double > &  energies,
double  resolution = 0 
)

Size of the distribution of one coordinate of the points (values, e.g. strip positions) from a gaussian fit of their energy, with the total energy as integral: centre and sigma are fitted by least squares.

  • Strips (resolution 0): the energy of each position (the hits at the same one added) is the gaussian integrated over its strip, of width the smallest distance between positions (the pitch), including the strips without energy between the first and last position and one beyond each; only the strips within 9 sigmas of the centre are evaluated (the others expect no energy). A gaussian evaluated at the strip centres (legacy) has no well defined minimum with a few strips; integrated over them, three strips with energy determine it (with two, any sigma below the pitch fits).
  • Continuous (resolution > 0, e.g. the drift distance, from the time of each pulse): each position is a point; the points and the gaussian are both smoothed with a gaussian of width resolution (without it, strips of width -> 0, a point with a third of the energy is fitted by sigma -> 0) and their least squares minimized, in a time that depends on the number of points. sigma is the width of the distribution without the resolution.

The minimization is deterministic (scans in log(sigma), from the pitch or the resolution / 100 to the extent, and in the centre, refined by golden section searches), without Minuit, so the result is the same on every platform.

Returns
The fitted sigma, 0 without points, -1 without result: fewer than three positions with more than 1% of the energy of the largest one, no positive total energy, or the best sigma at the extent of the points (the energy does not decrease away from the centre).

Origin and end of a track given by its points in order: the end is the extreme (first or last point) on the half of the track with more energy (the Bragg peak), the origin the other one. The halves split the projections of the points on the segment from the first to the last point (the point order for a single point or coinciding extremes). Using the energy of each half instead of the most energetic point (legacy) keeps a single noisy point from reversing the track.

Returns
The energy asymmetry of the halves, (E_end - E_origin) / (E_end + E_origin), from 0 (no preferred direction, origin = first point) to 1: how reliable the direction is. 0 without energy.
