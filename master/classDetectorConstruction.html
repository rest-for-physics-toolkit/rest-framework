Detailed Description

Builds the Geant4 geometry from the GDML file of the TRestGeant4Metadata.

The GDML is read with its entities and the parameters of the configuration resolved (TRestGDMLParser::WriteResolved). Construct checks that the physical and logical volume names are unique and that the sensitive, active, kill and generator volumes exist, sets the maximum step of the active volumes, the magnetic field (uniform, from the metadata) and the placement of the generator volume (gdml generators). ConstructSDandField attaches a SensitiveDetector to the logical volumes of the sensitive volumes.
