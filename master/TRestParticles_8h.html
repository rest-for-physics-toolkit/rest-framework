namespace  REST_Particles
 Particle names and IDs of REST: the ID of a particle is its PDG encoding (Monte Carlo particle numbering scheme, as Geant4 GetPDGEncoding), the one stored in the events (e.g. the tracks of TRestGeant4Event, the hits of TRestDetectorHitsEvent; 0 = unknown).
