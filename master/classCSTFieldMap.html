Detailed Description

Several field maps as one Garfield++ component, e.g. a fine map of the amplification structure (mesh, holes, dielectric) and a coarser map of the drift field above it, which cannot be one regular grid. At each point, the field and the medium of the first map (in the order of AddMap) whose mesh contains it; outside every map, status -6 and no medium.

Periodicity (Component::EnablePeriodicityX/Y/Z or EnableMirrorPeriodicityX/Y/Z, e.g. along x and y for the unit cell of a readout): the period is the extent of the box enclosing all the maps along that axis; a point is moved into it before choosing the map, and with mirror periodicity the field component along that axis changes sign in the mirrored cells (as ComponentGrid). The maps themselves are not periodic.
