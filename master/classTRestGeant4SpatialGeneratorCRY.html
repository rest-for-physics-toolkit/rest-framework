TRestGeant4SpatialGeneratorCRY Class Reference

type: "cry": the cosmic-ray showers of CRY, generated during the simulation, one per event with all its particles, with the options of the TRestGeant4CRY section (SetCRY). The CRY plane (z = 0, the square of subBoxLength) is centered at position and its +Z (up) is verticalAxis. Each copy (thread) has its own CRY, with the seed of Start plus its index (in the order of their first showers), and the showers and the exposure are the sums of all of them; the generator has no sources. With the world set (SetWorld, restG4), as the cosmic generator only the particles crossing the world are generated: a particle whose line misses the world box is removed, one starting outside it is moved along its direction to where it enters the world (its time delayed by the flight), and the showers without particles left are skipped (they count in the exposure of CRY, not in the events of Geant4).
