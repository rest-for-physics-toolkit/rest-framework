Detailed Description

type: "cosmic": the origin depends on the direction, a uniform point of the disk of radius radius through the world center normal to the direction, moved back to the sphere of that radius. The radius is, if not given, the one of the sphere circumscribing the world (written to the YAML). flux (counts / s / cm2 through the disk) is written when the source is loaded, if it is a distribution of energy and angle (TRestGeant4ParticleSource::GetFlux), to normalize to a time (TRestGeant4Metadata::GetCosmicExposureTime).
