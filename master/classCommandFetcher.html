Detailed Description

Reads the commands of femclient (interactive console or command file) and executes them on the FEMDAQ of the configuration.

Commands of femclient: fopen [all|root|log], fclose, addMetadata, startDAQ [flags], stopDAQ, Pedestals [flags], credits show|restore [frameThr reqFrames], fem <id>|*, verbose <level>, exec <file>, sleep <s>, quit or exit. Any other line is sent to the active FEMs (FEMDAQ::SendCommand). A failed command writes its error and the session continues.
