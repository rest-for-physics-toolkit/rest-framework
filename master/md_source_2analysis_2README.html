REST analysis (source/analysis)

libRestAnalysis holds the generic analysis algorithms of REST, written as free functions on standard containers (std::vector, ROOT::Math::XYZVector, TGraph, TH1) so that any event type or library can use them, and the dataset processes built on them. It depends on RestCore and RestTools.

Contents

Algorithms (namespaces)

  • TRestPulseShapeAnalysis (TRestPulseShapeAnalysis.h): signal parameters of pulses: baseline and its sigma (mean/SD, median/IQR or without outliers), maxima (point, triple max, gaussian, Landau or AGET fits), integrals, points over threshold, rise time and slope, smoothing, peak finding (GetPeaks), shaping responses and convolution, and generated noise.
  • TRestHitsAnalysis (TRestHitsAnalysis.h): sets of hits or track nodes: clustering (FindClusters), shortest path through the points (GetShortestPath), reduction to nodes (KMeansClustering, MergePoints), line fit and residual (GetLine, GetLineNodes, GetLineResidual), track ends (GetTrackEnds), gaussian sigma and twist.
  • TRestPeakFit (TRestPeakFit.h): RooFit fits of the peaks of a spectrum (gaussian or Crystal Ball, with an optional constant or linear background), peak search and fit windows, and the linear calibration fit.
  • TRestFFTAnalysis (TRestFFTAnalysis.h): FFT helpers on std::vector (forward/backward transforms through ROOT/FFTW), response spectra, low-pass filters and deconvolution. The in-place filters keep the Hermitian symmetry of the spectrum (ApplyLowPassFilter and KillFrequencies zero the same bins and their mirrors), RemoveBaseline sets the DC bin to 0, RenormalizeNode multiplies a bin by the factor and ButterWorthFilter throws std::invalid_argument for a cutOff of 0.

Dataset processes (TRestDataSetProcess, run by TRestDataSetProcessManager)

  • TRestDataSetCalibration: energy calibration of a dataset: fits the peaks of known energy of an observable in a reference dataset and adds the column <name>_<calibratedObservable> (linear in the observable). Its peaks are TRestDataSetCalibrationPeak.
  • TRestDataSetLogOdds: builds the PDF of observables in a reference dataset and adds their log odds <name>_<observable> and their sum <name>_total. Its observables are TRestDataSetLogOddsObservable.
  • TRestDataSetTMVA: trains a classifier of ROOT TMVA (a BDT by default) with the reference as signal and a background dataset, and adds its score <name>_score; the trained weights, the ROC integral, the separations and the overtraining tests are its results, the ROC curve and the score distributions its objects, drawn by DrawResults (macro REST_ViewTMVA of restRoot).
  • TRestDataSetSignalSelection: keeps the entries with an observable (e.g. logOdds_total, bdt_score) in the range holding a quantile interval of the reference (quantiles, e.g. [0, 0.8] or [0.2, 1]): fixes a signal efficiency on a calibration and applies it to a background (filter); its cuts (e.g. energy window and fiducial region) select the reference, and the dataset processed too with applyCuts: true or when it is the reference itself (by default a background is selected in the whole readout and at any energy).
  • TRestDataSetCombine: adds the columns of another dataset of the same events (combineFileName, e.g. the vetoes analysed apart, to the dataset of the Micromegas), matched by run, eventID and subEventID; the entries without match are kept with default values and <name>_matched 0 (keepUnmatched, true by default) or filtered out.

All are configured in YAML with their registered fields (see the class documentation) and store their results (fit parameters, PDFs) in their configuration, so a dataset processed with them can be used as the reference of later runs.

Use in the framework

  • TRestPulseShapeAnalysis: the raw and detector signal processes and events (libraries/raw, libraries/detector), e.g. TRestRawSignalAnalysisProcess, TRestRawSignalShapingProcess, TRestRawBaseLineCorrectionProcess, TRestDetectorSignalToHitsProcess.
  • TRestHitsAnalysis: the hits and track processes (libraries/detector, libraries/track), e.g. TRestDetectorHitsAnalysisProcess, TRestTrackReductionProcess, TRestTrackLineAnalysisProcess.
  • TRestPeakFit: TRestDataSetCalibration and TRestDataSetGainMap (libraries/detector).
  • TRestFFTAnalysis: available for signal processing; not used by the processes yet.

Examples

  • libraries/detector/examples/DataSetCalibration.yaml: calibration of datasets with TRestDataSetCalibration.
  • examples/DataSetLogOdds.yaml: log odds of calibrated datasets with TRestDataSetLogOdds.
  • examples/DataSetTMVA.yaml: BDT of calibrated datasets with TRestDataSetTMVA.
  • examples/DataSetSignalSelection.yaml: selection of the entries with the BDT score of the 80% of the calibration with TRestDataSetSignalSelection; examples/DataSetLogOddsSelection.yaml, with the log odds.
  • examples/DataSetTardis.yaml: time since the previous event with TRestDataSetTardis (libraries/detector).
