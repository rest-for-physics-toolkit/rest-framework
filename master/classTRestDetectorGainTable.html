Detailed Description

Avalanche gain of a medium versus the amplification field: the configuration of its tables (the gain key of a TRestDetectorTransportManager, computed by restGarfield) and one table (one condition and gap), read from or written to the medium file without Garfield++.

Configuration: one table per pressure and temperature, for an amplification gap with a uniform field (parallel plates, E = V / gap), over a grid of reduced field E/p (reducedFieldRange, fieldNodes equally spaced): the gain depends on E/p (and p * gap), so the working range in E/p is about the same at every pressure and each pressure p gets the fields E = (E/p) * p. The table is stored versus the field. restGarfield simulates avalanches microscopic avalanches per node (Garfield++ AvalancheMicroscopic), each one started by one electron at the mesh side of the gap; the table keeps the mean gain G and the Polya parameter theta from the moments of the avalanche sizes (sigma^2 / G^2 = 1 / (1 + theta)), and the distribution of the sizes per field. pressures and temperatures, if empty, are those of the medium. usePenning and penningRate as in TRestDetectorTransportTable. Tables that already exist (same key) are skipped unless overwrite is set.

gain: gap: 128 um reducedFieldRange: [25 kV/cm/bar, 35 kV/cm/bar] fieldNodes: 6 avalanches: 1000 usePenning: true penningRate: 0.3

Medium file: an entry per table in amplifications of the metadata of the medium (key, pressure, temperature, gap, fieldRange, penningRate, avalanches and generator) and, in amplification/<medium>/<key>/, the TGraphs <medium>_<key>_gain and <medium>_<key>_theta versus the field in V/cm and the TH1D <medium>_<key>_size_<i> of the avalanche sizes at the field node i. The key is the condition, gap and field range, e.g. 1.4bar_293.15K_gap128um_E25000-35000Vcm_Penning0.3. The gain does not scale with the pressure (it depends on E/p and p * gap): a table is only used at its pressure and temperature. The getters take the field in V/mm; log G and theta are interpolated linearly in the field, and a field out of the range of the table is an error.
