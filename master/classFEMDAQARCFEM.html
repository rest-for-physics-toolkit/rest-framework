Detailed Description

Data taking of ARC and FEMINOS FEMs (one or several), each with its own UDP socket.

Threads: a receiver thread per FEM, started by the constructor and running until the destructor (FEMReceiverThread: data frames to FEMProxy::tmpBuffer, other frames are command replies or monitoring); during startDAQ a request thread per active FEM (SendDAQCmdThread, request credits), the event builder (EventBuilder) and the update thread of FEMDAQ. The decoding of the frames is the one of ARCPacket or FEMINOSPacket (packetAPI).
