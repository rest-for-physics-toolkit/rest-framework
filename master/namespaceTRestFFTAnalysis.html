Detailed Description

Stateless FFT helpers working on std::vector, in the style of TRestPulseShapeAnalysis.

A spectrum is the full complex spectrum (N bins) of a real signal, so it keeps Hermitian symmetry: X[N-k] = conj(X[k]).
