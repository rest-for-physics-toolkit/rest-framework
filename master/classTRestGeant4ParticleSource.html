Detailed Description

A source of the generator: the particle (with excitedLevel and charge for an ion) and its energy and angular distributions, or a Decay0 file (use) which gives the particles. When energy and angular have the type formula2, TH2D or cosmic, they are sampled together here. See TRestGeant4PrimaryGenerator for the options. 

source:
  particle: "Pb210"         # Geant4 particle or ion name
  excitedLevel: 0 keV
  charge: 0
  energy:                   # TRestGeant4EnergyDistribution
    type: "mono"
    energy: 1 keV
  angular:                  # TRestGeant4AngularDistribution
    type: "isotropic"

or a Decay0 file, source: {use: "Xe136bb0n.dat"}, without particle, energy and angular.
