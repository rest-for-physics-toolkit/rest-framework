Detailed Description

Reduces the hits of each track (any type) to nodes, merging the hits closer than a growing distance up to a maximum number of nodes, optionally refined with k-means. The output event holds one track per input track.

Author
Javier Galán javie.nosp@m.r.ga.nosp@m.lan@u.nosp@m.niza.nosp@m.r.es
JuanAn García juana.nosp@m.ngp@.nosp@m.uniza.nosp@m.r.es

Reduces each track of a TRestTrackEvent, of any type (XZ, YZ, XYZ, ...), to nodes, merging its hits (TRestHitsAnalysis::MergePoints):

  1. The pairs of hits closer than startingDistance are merged (energy weighted position, summed energy) until there are none.
  2. The distance is multiplied by distanceStepFactor and step 1 repeated while it is below minimumDistance or the track has more than maxNodes nodes.
  3. With kMeans, the nodes are moved to their hits with k-means (each hit goes to its closest node, which moves to the energy weighted mean of its hits, up to maxIterations iterations, TRestHitsAnalysis::KMeansClustering, nodes with no hits dropped), and steps 1 to 3 are repeated on the nodes until their number does not change.

Each node gets the energy of its hits and, as sigma, their energy weighted spread in each coordinate (including the sigmas of the input hits, e.g. for tracks already reduced). The coordinates the track does not define stay NaN and the nodes have no time (0).

The output event holds one track per input track (parent ID = ID of the input track): the nodes, or the input track as it is if it has hits of different types (the undefined coordinates would mix with the defined ones). The number of tracks not reduced is reported at the end of the processing (and each one in debug).

Parameters

  • startingDistance: first merging distance (default 0.5 mm).
  • minimumDistance: the merging distance grows at least up to this distance (default 3 mm).
  • distanceStepFactor: factor applied to the merging distance at each step, > 1 (default 1.5).
  • maxNodes: maximum number of nodes of a track (default 30).
  • kMeans: refine the nodes with k-means (default false).
  • maxIterations: maximum k-means iterations (default 100).

Example:

trackReduction: class: "TRestTrackReductionProcess" startingDistance: 0.5mm minimumDistance: 3mm distanceStepFactor: 1.5 maxNodes: 30 kMeans: true maxIterations: 100

Migrated from the legacy track library (Javier Galan). Differences: kmeans and maxIt renamed kMeans and maxIterations; the node sigmas are the spread of their hits (legacy averaged the sigmas of the merged hits) and the nodes have no time (legacy energy weighted mean); the output event holds only the tracks of this stage (no levels).
