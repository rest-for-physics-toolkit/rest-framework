The geant4 library

This library is used to store and analyse the events generated in a Geant4 simulation. It defines and stores the particle generator and the simulation conditions, such as the geometry, the active volumes and the physics list used during the Monte Carlo, and implements processes on the simulated events (energy quenching, observables of volumes, particles and interactions).

The geant4 library (RestGeant4) holds the event and the metadata of the Geant4 simulations of rest-for-physics-toolkit: the event written by restG4 (primaries, energy per volume, tracks and hits), the simulation conditions (geometry, physics lists, active volumes, primary generator) and the processes that analyse the simulated events. It does not depend on Geant4: the parts that need it (filling the event from Geant4 steps, the volume tables of the Geant4 world, the positions in GDML volumes) are implemented in restG4. The conversion of the simulated hits into detector hits is done by the connectors library.

Units: energies in keV, lengths in mm, times in us and angles in rad; any YAML value can be given with units (e.g. 5.3 MeV, 2 cm, 30 deg).

Class Description
TRestGeant4Event Event of restG4: primaries, energy deposited in total, in the sensitive volumes, per volume and per volume, particle and process, and (with storeTracks) the tracks and their hits. Particles, processes and volumes are stored as IDs, named by the TRestGeant4Metadata of the run.
TRestGeant4EventData Flat storage of a TRestGeant4Event (one TTree branch per vector).

Simulation metadata

Class Description
TRestGeant4Manager Top section of a restG4 configuration: creates the run, the TRestGeant4Metadata, the TRestGeant4PhysicsLists and the TRestGeant4CRY, linked to the cry generator.
TRestGeant4CRY The options of CRY for the cry generator (subBoxLength, latitude, altitude, date, particles returned, maxExposureTime), a section of the TRestGeant4Manager.
TRestGeant4Metadata Simulation conditions (geometry, number of events, seed, stored energy range, active volumes with their storage chance, activateAllVolumes, sensitive and kill volumes, registerEmptyTracks, decay chain options, biasing volumes, generator) and the ID tables and biasing results (biasingTotalFactor, events and particles per stage) written by restG4; it keeps the geometry with the file. Normalize with GetEquivalentNumberOfEvents (nEvents without biasing).
TRestGeant4ActiveVolumeMetadata An entry of activeVolumes: volume expression, maximum step and sensitive flag.
TRestGeant4BiasingVolume An entry of biasingVolumes: a virtual box or sphere (not part of the geometry), an integer factor and an energy range. restG4 simulates in stages: the particles entering each volume (outermost first) are stopped and stored, and replayed biasingFactor times by the next stage; only the last stage is written, each event with weight 1 / biasingTotalFactor (see the restG4 README).
TRestGeant4GeometryInfo Geometry of the simulation (TGeoManager), volume ID table and volume lookup by regular expression.
TRestGeant4PhysicsInfo Particle and process ID tables (PDG encodings, Geant4 process type * 1000 + subtype).

Processes

TRestGeant4Event to TRestGeant4Event:

Class Description
TRestGeant4QuenchingProcess Quenches the hit energies only in the volumes given (volume, each with its model, lindhard or birks, required; model: the same model for all the other volumes of the geometry) and recomputes the event energies. The Lindhard model needs a simulation with storeHadronicTargetInfo: true (error otherwise).
TRestGeant4AnalysisProcess Observables of the event: global and primary ones and, per selection of volumes, particles and processes, energy, mean position, entering particle, energy per volume and interaction points (e.g. neutron captures). Times relative to the first energy deposit of the event (GetFirstDepositTime), as the hits of TRestGeant4ToDetectorHitsProcess.

The configuration keys, the observables and a YAML example of each process are given in its class documentation.
