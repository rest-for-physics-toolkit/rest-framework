Detailed Description

Adds the energy deposited in the sensitive volumes to the event (TRestGeant4Event sensitive volume energy).

It is attached to logical volumes, but only the steps in the physical volumes declared sensitive in the TRestGeant4Metadata are counted. For geantinos the step length (mm) is added as energy (keV).
