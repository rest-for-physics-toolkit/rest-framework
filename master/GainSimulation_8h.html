Computes the tables of a TRestDetectorGainTable configuration with Garfield++: for each condition and field node, simulates microscopic avalanches (AvalancheMicroscopic) in a uniform field across the gap (parallel plates), each one started by one electron at the mesh side, and adds the table (mean gain, Polya theta from the moments and the distribution of the avalanche sizes) to the medium file. Only gases.

The conditions are the pressures and temperatures of the configuration (by default those of the medium); the fields are the reduced field nodes (E/p) times each pressure. A table already in the medium file is skipped unless overwrite is set. With usePenning, the Penning transfer (penningRate) is enabled.

Parameters
mediumMedium definition (a gas); its mediumFile receives the tables.
simulationConfiguration of the gain tables (the gain section of TRestDetectorTransportManager).
verbosePrint the gain and theta of each field node.
Exceptions
std::runtime_errorif the medium is not a gas, its gas cannot be created, the Penning transfer cannot be enabled or the medium file cannot be opened.
