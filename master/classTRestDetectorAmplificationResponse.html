Detailed Description

Amplification of the electrons in the field map of a unit cell of the amplification structure (e.g. a microbulk Micromegas exported by CST): the configuration of its computation (the amplification key of a TRestDetectorTransportManager, computed by restGarfield with Garfield++ microscopic avalanches) and the response (transparency, avalanche size and time), written to and read from its own file without Garfield++, used by TRestDetectorAvalancheProcess (responseFile).

The parameters (YAML keys), the file format and the use of the response are described in the class documentation (TRestDetectorAmplificationResponse.cxx).

Author
JuanAn García juana.nosp@m.ngp@.nosp@m.uniza.nosp@m.r.es

Amplification of the drifted electrons in a non-uniform field (the field map of a unit cell of the amplification structure, e.g. a microbulk Micromegas exported from CST Studio Suite) as a response, for special simulations; the amplification in a uniform field across the gap (parallel plates) is the gain table of the medium (TRestDetectorGainTable). Both are used by TRestDetectorAvalancheProcess.

restGarfield computes it (the amplification key of a TRestDetectorTransportManager, after the drift responses): it loads the field maps in Garfield++ (restGarfield LoadCSTFieldMaps: the first map takes precedence where they overlap; the nodes with the field 0, the conductors, and the dielectric nodes of the D maps stop the electrons), repeats the cell along the periodicity axes (simple or mirror) and simulates the microscopic avalanches (AvalancheMicroscopic, the Garfield gas of the composition of the medium at its pressure and temperature, Magboltz cross sections, with the Penning transfer if usePenning) of electrons primary electrons started at random over the cell (x and y) on the plane z = start, in the frame of the maps moved by fieldMapOffset. The electrons drift towards -z; an electron of the avalanche ending below z = anode is collected by the anode. A primary whose avalanche brings no electron to the anode is lost (on the mesh, on a dielectric, attached); the transparency is the fraction of the primaries with an avalanche at the anode. Where the lost primaries ended is counted (Losses, from the first electron of the avalanche that stopped): out of the maps or the cell (e.g. back above the start plane), on a conductor (the nodes with the field 0 next to its end point, e.g. the mesh), on a dielectric (the dielectric nodes next to it, e.g. the kapton), attached, or other. start is the boundary plane of the drift response (TRestDetectorDriftResponse boundary, measured from the mesh): the drift response ends there and the amplification starts there, so the electrons lost on the mesh are counted once.

Response: the number of primaries and of collected avalanches, the histogram of the size of the collected avalanches (electrons at the anode, sizeBins over [0, the largest size]) with its mean and variance, and the histogram of the time from the start plane to the anode (the mean time of the electrons of the avalanche at the anode, timeBins over the range of the times). The map of the cell (cellBins x cellBins bins over x and y of the cell, by the start point of the primaries): the transparency and the mean size of each bin, e.g. the losses over the solid mesh around the holes. The electrons at the anode out of the cell where their primary started (on the pad of another cell) are counted: in a symmetric cell the field lines of a cell end on its pad, and those electrons crossed the cell edge by diffusion below the start plane (in the last part of the drift and in the gap). GetSharingSigma gives the sigma of the Gaussian displacement along x and y that crosses the edges with that fraction, f = 2 sigma (1 / sizeX + 1 / sizeY) / sqrt(2 pi) (e.g. 5.5 % of a cell of 0.675 mm: 23 um). TRestDetectorAvalancheProcess with responseFile uses it for each drifted electron: it passes with the transparency, its gain is sampled from the size histogram and its time is delayed by a time sampled from the time histogram. With the map, the transparency is that of the bin of the electron in the cell and the gain is scaled by the mean size of the bin over the mean of all; the cell is the volume of the readout containing the electron (a pixel, TRestDetectorReadout::GetPixelPosition): its x and y from the lower corner, as fractions of its size, are those of the cell (so the cell must be centred on the pixel, with its size). The positions of the amplified hits are displaced in the readout plane by GetSharingSigma (charge sharing between pads).

Parameters (amplification key): fieldMaps (required), dielectricMaps ([]: none; one per field map in the same order, "" for a map without), fieldMapOffset ([0, 0, 0] mm), periodicity ("xy"), mirror (false), start and anode (z in mm, start > anode), electrons (1000), usePenning (false), penningRate (0), sizeBins (100), timeBins (50), cellBins (10; 0: no map), responseFile and overwrite (false: an existing responseFile is not computed again). The gain factor of a bin has a statistical error of about 1 / sqrt((1 + theta) T n) for n primaries in the bin (~0.7 / sqrt(n) for theta 1.7, T 0.8), applied by TRestDetectorAvalancheProcess as a false non-uniformity: ~500 primaries per bin (3 %, transparency to +-0.02), electrons ~ 500 cellBins^2. One response per operating point (the voltages of the CST model: mesh voltage and drift field).

amplification: fieldMaps: ["pixel_mesh320_E_fine.txt", "pixel_mesh320_E_coarse.txt"] dielectricMaps: ["pixel_mesh320_D_fine.txt"] periodicity: "xy" start: 1 mm anode: -54 um electrons: 2000 responseFile: "amplification_320V.root"

Response file: the metadata AmplificationResponse in RESTMetadataStore (YAML: configuration, the amplification keys; medium, pressure, temperature, penning, primaries, collected, losses (out, conductor, dielectric, attached, other), meanSize, sizeVariance, sizeMax, sizeCounts, tMin, tMax, timeCounts, cellLow, cellHigh, cellPrimaries, cellCollected, cellSizeSum, anodeElectrons, sharedElectrons and generator, the program, date and code that computed it), the histograms TH1D AmplificationSize and AmplificationTime (us) and the map of the cell, TH2D AmplificationTransparencyMap and AmplificationGainMap (mean size), to draw them.
