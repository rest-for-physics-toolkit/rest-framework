Detailed Description

Converts the energy deposits of a TRestDetectorHitsEvent into ionization electrons and drifts them to the readout (position on the readout, arrival time) through a TRestDetectorMedium at its drift field, or with a drift response computed by restGarfield in a field map (TRestDetectorDriftResponse, responseFile; replaces the legacy TRestDetectorElectronDiffusionProcess and TRestDetectorGarfieldDriftProcess).

The parameters (YAML keys), the observables and an example are described in the class documentation (TRestDetectorHitsDriftProcess.cxx).

Author

Damien Neyret
JuanAn García juana.nosp@m.ngp@.nosp@m.uniza.nosp@m.r.es

Converts the energy of each hit into ionization electrons (energy / wValue of the medium, with the chosen fluctuations) and diffuses each electron with the TRestDetectorMedium at its drift field (driftField of its charge; gaussian diffusion and attachment, from a transport table of the medium or its fixed parameters; the values used are written to the metadata of the process) and drifts it to the readout. Each surviving electron becomes a hit on the readout (its diffused position projected on the readout plane, TRestDetectorReadout::ProjectToReadout, where the readout gives its channel) at its arrival time, the time of the original hit plus its diffused distance to the readout over the drift velocity of the medium, with the energy of its electrons: the electrons of a hit carry the fluctuated number of electrons times the W-value (the energy of the hit without fluctuations), so the fluctuation is kept. A hit whose number of electrons fluctuates to 0 or less is removed. TRestDetectorHitsToSignalProcess then gives the signals; the drift coordinate is recovered from the time by TRestDetectorHitsTimeProjectionProcess (e.g. to look at the drifted electrons in 3D).

With diffusion: false the hits are not split into electrons nor diffused: each hit is projected whole on the readout at t + d / v, d its distance to the readout, with its charge fluctuated as above and attenuated by the mean attachment, exp(-attachment * d) (faster, e.g. to check the geometry and the readout; maxHits is not used).

The drift distance and its direction are given by TRestDetectorReadout::GetDistanceToReadout (e.g. the Micromegas plane and its normal). Hits without a distance or outside the drift volume (distance < 0 or beyond TRestDetectorReadout::GetMaxDistanceToReadout, the cathode) are removed. Diffused electrons are kept between the readout plane and the cathode, as legacy. A readout without maximum distance (NaN, e.g. a Micromegas readout without gasThickness) has no cathode: no upper cut.

In a non-uniform field (special simulations), responseFile is a drift response computed by restGarfield in a field map (TRestDetectorDriftResponse, the drift key of a TRestDetectorTransportManager) and replaces the drift field of the medium (its transport is not used, only wValue and fanoFactor): each electron survives with the fraction of the electrons that reach the boundary plane from the position of its hit (TRestDetectorDriftResponse::GetSurvival, interpolated between the nodes of the grid); a surviving electron becomes a hit on the readout (the hit projected on the readout plane plus the displacement sampled from the response along the axes of the readout plane, TRestDetectorDriftResponse::PlaneAxes of the readout normal) at the time of the hit plus the drift time sampled from the response (SampleArrival). The arrival is on the readout plane at the time of the boundary plane: the drift from the boundary to the readout (through the mesh) belongs to the amplification (TRestDetectorAvalancheProcess with an amplification response). Hits outside the grid of the response are removed; the cuts of the drift volume are those of the response (its grid and its field map). diffusion: false is not available with a response.

Differences with legacy: the attachment is exp(-attachment * distance) with attachment in 1/cm (as Garfield), the gas parameters come from the medium instead of the process, there are no veto planes (veto hits are split beforehand) and there is no microscopic Garfield drift: in a uniform field it uses the same transport table as the gaussian diffusion (microscopic simulations are done with restGarfield).

Parameters: readout (empty: the data readout of the TRestDetectorReadoutManager), medium (empty: the only one with charge), fluctuations ("fano", "poisson" or "none"), maxHits (1000, 0: no limit), diffusion (true), responseFile (empty: the uniform drift field of the medium), seed (0: random seed; otherwise the generator is re-seeded at each event from the seed and the event and subevent IDs, TRestEvent::EventSeed, so the result does not depend on the number of threads).

Example:

hitsDrift: class: "TRestDetectorHitsDriftProcess" maxHits: 1000

In a field map:

hitsDrift: class: "TRestDetectorHitsDriftProcess" responseFile: "drift_response.root" maxHits: 1000
