Detailed Description

Collects the hits of a TRestDetectorHitsEvent in the readout channels: a TRestDetectorSignalEvent with the charge (energy) of each channel versus the time of its hits (generic: no drift).

The parameters (YAML keys), the observables and an example are described in the class documentation (TRestDetectorHitsToSignalProcess.cxx).

Author
Javier Galán javie.nosp@m.r.ga.nosp@m.lan@u.nosp@m.niza.nosp@m.r.es
JuanAn García juana.nosp@m.ngp@.nosp@m.uniza.nosp@m.r.es

Converts a TRestDetectorHitsEvent into a TRestDetectorSignalEvent: the energy of each hit is added to the signal of the readout channel at its position (TRestDetectorReadout::GetChannelFromPosition), at the time of the hit. It is generic: the hits must already be where and when they are collected, e.g. the electrons drifted to the readout plane at their arrival time (TRestDetectorHitsDriftProcess) or the deposits of a scintillator read as a whole. Hits without channel are rejected and counted in the observable NHitsRejected.

With sampling > 0 the times are rounded down to multiples of it (fewer points per signal); otherwise the times are exact. Events without signals are rejected.

Differences with legacy: the drift (projection on the readout, drift time and drift volume cuts) is done by TRestDetectorHitsDriftProcess, there is no light-speed time for veto channels (they go in their own pipeline; the light has its own processes), no readout planes loop and the sampling is optional.

Parameters: readout (empty: the data readout of the TRestDetectorReadoutManager), sampling (0).

Example:

hitsToSignal: class: "TRestDetectorHitsToSignalProcess" observables:

  • "NHitsRejected"
