Detailed Description

Online event display of raw signal files (formerly the EventDisplay macro of FEMDAQ): for each readout, the amplitude spectrum, the hit map and the signals of the event, plus the rate.

It reads the tree TRestRawSignalEvent of a REST file (e.g. written by femclient, package restDAQ) and, in the live mode, follows the file while it is written (TTree::Refresh, it sees the events of the last AutoSave of femclient) and opens the next subrun or run (Run<run>_..._<subrun>.root, first subrun 000) when "Auto-load Next Run" is checked. Launched with the macro REST_DAQViewer(configFile, dataFile) of restRoot.

The configuration is a YAML of restv2, e.g. the one of the analysis with restManager; the viewer takes from it (at any depth):

  • TRestDetectorReadoutManager: the readouts (inputFileName, instanceName, decodingName). Each readout is drawn separately with the signals of its decoding. Its hit map, chosen automatically:

    • strips on two axes (TRestDetectorReadout::GetPositionFromChannel gives a single coordinate, e.g. x or y of a Micromegas, not necessarily X and Y): the amplitude-weighted mean of the strips of each axis.
    • otherwise, the volumes of the channels in the geometry of the readout (e.g. the scintillators of a veto readout, TRestGDMLReadout): their projections on the XY, XZ and YZ planes, each volume a bin with the number of events with a signal in its channel (volumes overlapping in a projection are drawn one over the other).

    Without readouts, all the signals in a single view without hit map.

  • TRestRawSignalAnalysisProcess: baselineRange, baselineOption, integralRange, pointsThreshold, signalThreshold, pointsOverThreshold, nPointsFlat and maxOption. The amplitude of a signal is its AmpSgnMaxMethod (a signal with at least 2 points over threshold); the spectrum of a readout is the sum of the amplitudes of its signals in the event. Without it, the default values of the process.
  • parameters (the section parameters of restv2, also with –set): the binning of the spectra, spectrumBins, spectrumMin and spectrumMax (default 2048, 0 and 100000), and for a readout spectrumBins_<instanceName>, spectrumMin_<instanceName> and spectrumMax_<instanceName> (the others by default). A parameter not used (e.g. a readout misspelt) is warned.

The readout files are relative to the current directory. The settings of the window (files, step) are kept in $REST_HOME/.rest/DAQViewer_settings (~/.rest without REST_HOME).
