Detailed Description

Transforms the hits of a TRestGeant4Event into the hits of a TRestDetectorHitsEvent, only those of the selected geometry volumes (the sensitive ones by default).

Author
Igor G. Irastorza
Javier Galán javie.nosp@m.r.ga.nosp@m.lan@u.nosp@m.niza.nosp@m.r.es
JuanAn García juana.nosp@m.ngp@.nosp@m.uniza.nosp@m.r.es

Transfers the hits (position, energy, time) of a TRestGeant4Event with positive energy to a TRestDetectorHitsEvent as XYZ hits, with the Particle ID (PDG encoding) of the track of each hit (fHitParticleID, e.g. for the scintillation profile of each particle). Only the hits of the volumes selected as in TRestGeant4QuenchingProcess are transferred (TRestGeant4GeometryInfo::GetVolumesMatchingExpression: physical volumes matching each regular expression or, if none, those of the matching logical volumes); without volumes, those of the sensitive volumes of the simulation (volumes: [".*"] or ["^."] for all of them).

The times of the hits are relative to the first energy deposit of the Geant4 event in any volume (TRestGeant4Event::GetFirstDepositTime, the same origin as the times of TRestGeant4AnalysisProcess and for every detector of the event): the times of a radioactive decay, ~1e13 us after the generation of the event, become those of the detector (a trigger at the first deposit, drift and scintillation times of ns or us). The time of that first deposit is the observable TimeOffset (the event time is not changed: it is the generation time of the event).

Difference with legacy: there is no type per volume (legacy VETO hits), the hits of different detectors are transferred by separate processes or pipelines with their own volumes.

Parameters: volumes ([], the sensitive volumes), list of regular expressions (e.g. "gas" for any name containing gas, "^gas" for the names starting with it).

Example:

geant4ToHits: class: "TRestGeant4ToDetectorHitsProcess" volumes:

  • "gasVolume"
  • "^scintillatorVolume"
