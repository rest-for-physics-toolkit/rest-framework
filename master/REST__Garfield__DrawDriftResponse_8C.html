TCanvas * REST_Garfield_DrawDriftResponse ( const std::string &  fileName,
double  z = -1

Draws a drift response computed by restGarfield (TRestDetectorDriftResponse, the drift section): what the electric field does to the electrons on their way to the readout, from the summary of each start point of the grid (TRestDetectorDriftResponse::GetNodeSummary). Maps on the readout plane of the layer of start points at the distance z (by default the farthest layer, where the field acts longest):

  • survival: fraction of the electrons that reach the readout (lost at the walls, the field shapers, out of the gas or attached);
  • displacement: mean shift of the arrival from the start point projected on the readout (u, v of the readout plane; x, y for a normal along z), as a map of its size with arrows (scaled, see the title); 0 in a uniform field, the distortion of the field otherwise;
  • radial displacement versus the radius of the start point: positive, the electrons are pushed outwards;
  • mean drift time;
  • drift time versus z at the centre of the grid and at its edges along x (the drift velocity is the inverse of the slope, constant in a uniform field);
  • transverse spread (the standard deviation of the arrival, the diffusion), sqrt(sigmaU^2 + sigmaV^2).

The tree DriftResponse of the file has the same summary per start point to draw other things, e.g. DriftResponse->Draw("meanU:x", "z == 90 && abs(y) < 5").

restRoot REST_Garfield_DrawDriftResponse("AlphaCAMM_drift.root") REST_Garfield_DrawDriftResponse("AlphaCAMM_drift.root", 45) // the layer closest to z = 45 mm

Parameters
fileNameResponse file (~ is expanded to HOME).
zCoordinate (mm) of the layer of start points along the third axis of the grid (z); negative: the last layer.
Returns
The canvas (3x2 pads), or nullptr if the file cannot be read.
