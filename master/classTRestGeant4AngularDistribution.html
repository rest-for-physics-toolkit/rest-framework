Detailed Description

Direction of the primaries of a source (the angular section): the type selects the derived class, which reads its own fields. direction is the fixed direction or the reference of the angle (as legacy, the reference points to the center for a sphere surface). Angles in rad. See TRestGeant4PrimaryGenerator for the options. 

angular:
  type: "isotropic"           # flux, isotropic, formula, TH1D, formula2, TH2D or cosmic
  direction: [0, -1, 0]
  isotropicConeHalfAngle: 20 deg
