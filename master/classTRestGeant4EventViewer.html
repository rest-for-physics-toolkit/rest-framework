Detailed Description

3D TEve viewer of a TRestGeant4Event, used by TRestBrowser (see REST_Geant4_ViewEvent).

AddEvent draws each track with initial kinetic energy >= 1 keV and length >= 0.1 mm as a line through its hits, nested under its parent track, coloured by particle (gamma green, e- red, neutron orange, alpha yellow, mu- gray, other negative magenta and positive azure, geantino dashed red), with a width growing with the initial energy and dashed if created by nCapture; and the hits as points grouped by process type and process name (Electromagnetic processes red, Transportation and Init white, hadElastic orange, neutronInelastic green, nCapture blue). It needs the TRestGeant4Metadata of the event (GetGeant4Metadata) for the names of the IDs.
