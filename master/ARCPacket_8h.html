Detailed Description

Decoding of the frames of the ARC electronics (AGET/AFTER).

The frames are sequences of 16-bit words; each word starts with a prefix that gives its type (start of frame, start/end of event, channel index, time bin, ADC sample, ASCII message...) and the content in the remaining bits.
