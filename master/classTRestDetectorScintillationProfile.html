Detailed Description

Time profile of the scintillation of some particles: its components (TRestDetectorScintillationComponent, fractions adding up to 1) and the particles it applies to (REST_Particles names: e-, alpha, Ar40..., or "ion" for any nucleus; none: the default profile, for the other particles and the hits without particle, e.g. data). One entry of the scintillation list of a TRestDetectorLightResponse.
