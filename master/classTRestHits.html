void DrawHistograms (TVirtualPad *pad, const std::string &histOption="", double pitch=0, bool markers3D=false) const
 Draws the hits as histograms weighted by energy in the pad: a row with the projections (XZ, YZ, XY) with hits defining both coordinates, plus a 3D view when the hits define the three coordinates, and a row with the X, Y and Z distributions. Empty histograms are not drawn, so 1D, 2D and 3D hits only get the pads that apply. The bin size is the pitch or, if pitch <= 0, the minimum distance between hits in X and Y (the readout pitch), also used for Z. If it gives more than 200 bins over the hits range, the coordinate is taken as continuous (e.g. simulated hits): bins of 1 mm without pitch, and at most 200 bins per axis. The 3D view shows a TH3 weighted by energy with the hits defining the three coordinates, and the hits with one undefined coordinate (e.g. strips) as lines along it, coloured by energy, with the crossings of lines along different coordinates (the reconstructed positions, ghosts included). With markers3D (track view), the Z bin size is the spacing of the samples of the pulses (median distance between consecutive Z values of the same channel, if any channel has several), and the 3D view shows instead markers (TPolyMarker3D) colored by energy with the palette, on an empty TH3 for the axes: the hits defining the three coordinates and the hits with one undefined coordinate combined in each bin of their common coordinate, both projections sorted by their defined coordinate and paired monotonically by their accumulated energy fraction (no ghost combinations), the direction given by the sign of the slopes of both projections along the common coordinate (e.g. alpha tracks with XZ and YZ strips).

Detailed Description

Lightweight view and utility API over TRestHitsData.

The class references external storage and exposes geometric/statistical operations for subsets of hits. A view covers the hits [fStartIdx, fStartIdx + fNHits) of the storage (e.g. one track of a track event sharing the storage of all its tracks). Hits are added at the end of the storage and removed from it, so the methods that add or remove hits (AddHit, AddHits, RemoveHit, RemoveHits, MergeHits) are only allowed on the last view of the storage (the one ending at its end, e.g. a single view over the whole storage): on any other view they throw std::logic_error, as the hits would fall outside the view and the indices of the following views would shift.

The energy-weighted means, sigmas and skewness of a coordinate only use the hits defining it. The distances use the plane of the hits if all are XY, XZ or YZ, otherwise the three coordinates.
