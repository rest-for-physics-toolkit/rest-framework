Detailed Description

Gain map of a readout: calibrates each segment of the readout to correct its gain inhomogeneities. See the class documentation in TRestDataSetGainMap.cxx.

Author
Álvaro Ezquerro aezqu.nosp@m.erro.nosp@m.@uniz.nosp@m.ar.e.nosp@m.s
JuanAn García juana.nosp@m.ngp@.nosp@m.uniza.nosp@m.r.es

Gain map of a readout, a process of TRestDataSetProcessManager: corrects the gain inhomogeneities of big readouts (e.g. TREX-DM) by calibrating each segment. The extent of the readout in the two axes of its plane (the bounding box of its channels, TRestDetectorReadout::GetChannelsBoundingBox; the normal is the axis of smallest extent, X and Y of the map the two others in cyclic order: X and Y for a plane XY, Y and Z for a normal X, Z and X for a normal Y) is divided in segments, and the events of a segment are those with the spatial observables inside it. The readout is the one of the reference dataset (its TRestDetectorReadoutManager, TRestDataSet::GetMetadataOfType). In the reference (referenceFileName with the cuts, see TRestDataSetProcess), the peaks of known energy are fitted in the spectrum of observable of each segment and of the whole readout, and the energy against their position is fitted with a line. The columns added, for all the events, are:

  • <name>_<calibratedObservable> (e.g. gainMap_Energy): calibrated with the segment of the event, slope * observable + intercept (keV); NaN (not calibrated) if a spatial observable of the event is not finite (e.g. no position); outside the edges, the first or last segment;
  • <name>_<calibratedObservable>NoSegmentation: calibrated with the whole readout.

A detector with several readouts (e.g. the two sides of TREX-DM) has a gain map per readout, each one on its own dataset (generated with the cuts of the events of that readout).

The fit window of each peak is its range if it is given; otherwise ±window (20%) of its position estimated from the maximum of the spectrum, which should be the first peak (position = energy * maximum / first energy). The fits are made with TRestPeakFit (RooFit): a gaussian (as legacy) or a Crystal Ball, optionally with a constant or linear background under the peak.

Parameters

  • observable: the observable calibrated.
  • calibratedObservable: name of the calibrated observable in the columns ("Energy", as TRestDataSetCalibration).
  • spatialObservableX, spatialObservableY: observables of the position of the segmentation, in the frame of the readout.
  • spatialObservableXSecondary, spatialObservableYSecondary (optional): an event of a segment of the fit must also have them inside the segment.
  • readout: the readout (instance in the TRestDetectorReadoutManager of the reference); empty: the data readout of the manager (its readout, or the only one).
  • peaks: energy (keV by default) and optionally range of each peak; the first one should be the maximum of the spectrum.
  • range: range of the observable of the spectra; automatic if not given (up to where the last 1% of the spectrum starts, as legacy).
  • nBins: number of bins of the spectra (100).
  • segments: number of segments in X and Y ([1, 1]), of equal size in the extent of the readout.
  • splitX, splitY: edges of the segments (instead of segments and the readout), e.g. [0, 50, 246].
  • zeroPoint: add the point (0, 0) to the linear fits (false; added anyway if there is only one peak).
  • peakModel: "gaussian" (default, as legacy) or "crystalBall" (low energy tail).
  • background: "none" (default, as legacy), "constant" or "linear" under each peak.
  • window: half width of the fit window of the peaks without range, relative to their estimated position (0.2).
  • peakSearch: search each peak in its range (or its estimated window) and fit it in ±window around the peak found, inside the range (false); useful when the gain of the segments differs. The search (TRestPeakFit::FindPeak) only accepts significant peaks (prominence over 3 times the statistical fluctuation, smoothed spectrum): if there is none, the peak is fitted in the whole range.
  • referenceFileName, cuts: see TRestDataSetProcess.

Results

splitX, splitY (edges of the segments), slope and intercept of each segment ([x][y]), fullSlope and fullIntercept of the whole readout; referenceUsed and parametersFileName (see TRestDataSetProcess).

Objects (folder RESTDataSetStore/TRestDataSetGainMap/<name> of the output dataset): spectrum and spectrum_<x>_<y> (with the peak fits g0, g1..., drawn with them), linearFit and linearFit_<x>_<y>.

Review and refit (restRoot)

The gain map of a dataset processed by it is loaded with ReadFromFile; after a refit, UpdateFile saves it in the dataset (its configuration and objects; the columns are not recomputed: run again the pipeline with this dataset as referenceFileName, the gain map is read from it):

TRestDataSetGainMap gainMap; gainMap.ReadFromFile("Dataset_Calibration_Calibrated.root", "gainMap"); gainMap.DrawSpectrum(); gainMap.DrawGainMap(); gainMap.Refit(0, 0, 0, {100000, 200000}); // segment [0][0], first peak, new range gainMap.DrawSegmentSpectrum(0, 0); gainMap.UpdateFile("Dataset_Calibration_Calibrated.root");

Example (in a TRestDataSetProcessManager):

gainMap: class: "TRestDataSetGainMap" referenceFileName: "Dataset_Calibration.root" observable: "rawAnalysis_ThresholdIntegral" spatialObservableX: "trackAnalysis_MaxTrack_XZ_MeanX" spatialObservableY: "trackAnalysis_MaxTrack_YZ_MeanY" segments: [5, 5] peaks:

  • {energy: "22.5 keV", range: [250000, 370000]}
  • {energy: "8 keV"}

Differences with legacy (TRestDataSetGainMap): a process of a dataset pipeline (no GenerateGainMap, CalibrateDataSet, Import/Export nor calibFileName/outputFileName: referenceFileName, ReadFromFile and UpdateFile); the input is a dataset (not run files); a single readout, without modules (no planeId, moduleId nor definitionCut: a gain map per readout, each one on its dataset), whose extent comes from the readout of the dataset (legacy: readoutRange, default [-1, 246.24]); the columns are <name>_<calibratedObservable> and <name>_<calibratedObservable>NoSegmentation (legacy: <name>_<observable>, _NoSegmentation and <name>_pmID); the segment of the column is clamped silently to the first or last segment (legacy warned for every event); the zero point is kept in the refits; the gain map is drawn with the edges of the segments; no REST_RefitGainMap macro nor excludeColumns (all the columns are written); no peak search with TSpectrum (a legacy ROOT interface) nor autoRangePeaks: windows from the maximum of the spectrum (the first peak instead of the highest energy) or the ranges, and the peaks fitted with RooFit (TRestPeakFit), optionally with a Crystal Ball and a background.
