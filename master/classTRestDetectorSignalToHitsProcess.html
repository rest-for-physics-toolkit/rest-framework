Detailed Description

Transforms the signals of a TRestDetectorSignalEvent into the hits of a TRestDetectorHitsEvent using the readout (channel position) and the pulses (energy and time); generic, no drift.

The parameters (YAML keys), the observables and an example are described in the class documentation (TRestDetectorSignalToHitsProcess.cxx).

Author
Javier Galán javie.nosp@m.r.ga.nosp@m.lan@u.nosp@m.niza.nosp@m.r.es
JuanAn García juana.nosp@m.ngp@.nosp@m.uniza.nosp@m.r.es

Creates the hits of an event from its signals. It is generic: the channel of each signal gives the coordinates defined by the readout (TRestDetectorReadout::GetPositionFromChannel, e.g. only X or only Y for strips, the centre of a scintillator; undefined ones are NaN) and the pulse gives the energy and the time of the hits. The hit type (X, Y, XY, ...) is deduced from the coordinates that are defined. In a detector with drift, the coordinate along the drift is obtained from the time afterwards, by TRestDetectorHitsTimeProjectionProcess.

Methods (energy, time of each hit):

  • onlyMax, tripleMax, tripleMaxAverage, gaussFit, landauFit, agetFit: one hit at the pulse maximum. With smoothingPoints > 1, onlyMax, tripleMax and tripleMaxAverage take the maximum of the signal smoothed with a moving average of that number of points (TRestPulseShapeAnalysis::GetSignalSmoothed; the fits already average the noise), with the energy of the smoothed signal.
  • integral: one hit with the charge integrated in integralRange (whole signal if empty) at the time of the maximum in that range. For pulses without drift (scintillators, solid state, bolometers).
  • qCenter: one hit at the charge-weighted mean time, energy = mean charge per point (as legacy).
  • all: one hit per signal point.
  • intWindow: one hit per integration window of intWindow, if the mean charge is over threshold.

Differences with legacy: no driftVelocity (TRestDetectorHitsTimeProjectionProcess).

Parameters: readout (empty: the data readout of the TRestDetectorReadoutManager), method ("tripleMaxAverage"), smoothingPoints (5), intWindow (5), threshold (100), integralRange ([0, 0]).

Example:

signalToHits: class: "TRestDetectorSignalToHitsProcess" method: "tripleMaxAverage"
