Detailed Description

Kinetic energy of the primaries of a source (the energy section): the type selects the derived class, which reads its own fields. Energies in keV. See TRestGeant4PrimaryGenerator for the options. 

energy:
  type: "flat"                # mono, flat, log, formula, TH1D, formula2, TH2D or cosmic
  range: [1 MeV, 10 MeV]
